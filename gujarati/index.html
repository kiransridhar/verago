Learn Gujarati ગુજરાતી

Gujarati is the language of Gujarat, spoken by more than 50 million people in India and by large Gujarati communities in the UK, East Africa and North America. It has its own script, close to Devanagari but without the top line.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Gujarati words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Gujarati script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
