How to say “How are you?” in Gujarati

“How are you?” in Gujarati is Kem chho (કેમ છો). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Kem chhoકેમ છો

In the Verago app this is taught as “How are you”.

It is one of the 15 words in Gujarati greetings. The letters under the word are Gujarati script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
