How to say “I don’t know” in Gujarati

“I don’t know” in Gujarati is Khabar nathi (ખબર નથી). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Khabar nathiખબર નથી

It is one of the 15 words in Gujarati everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Gujarati script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
