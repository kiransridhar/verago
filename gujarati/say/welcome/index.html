How to say “Welcome” in Gujarati

“Welcome” in Gujarati is Svaagat (સ્વાગત). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Svaagatસ્વાગત

Say it in 2 parts: Svaa · gat

It is one of the 15 words in Gujarati polite phrases. The letters under the word are Gujarati script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
