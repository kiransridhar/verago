How to say “Happy Birthday” in Gujarati

Happy Birthday in Gujarati is Janmdivas mubaarak (જન્મદિવસ મુબારક). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

The Gujarati word for birthday is janmdivas, "birth day".

“Happy birthday”

Janmdivas mubaarakજન્મદિવસ મુબારક

  1. Janmdivasજન્મદિવસbirthday
  2. mubaarakમુબારકblessed, happy

“Good wishes for your birthday”

Janmdivas-ni shubhkaamnaaoજન્મદિવસની શુભકામનાઓ

A little more formal.

  1. Janmdivas-niજન્મદિવસનીof the birthday
  2. shubhkaamnaaoશુભકામનાઓgood wishes
