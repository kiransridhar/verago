How to say “Happy New Year” in Gujarati

Happy New Year in Gujarati is Navu varsh mubaarak (નવું વર્ષ મુબારક). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

These wishes work on January 1. The Gujarati new year itself, Bestu Varas, is the day after Diwali, and on that day people greet each other with "Saal Mubaarak".

“Happy New Year”

Navu varsh mubaarakનવું વર્ષ મુબારક

  1. Navuનવુંnew
  2. varshવર્ષyear
  3. mubaarakમુબારકblessed, happy

“Good wishes for the new year”

Nava varsh-ni shubhkaamnaaoનવા વર્ષની શુભકામનાઓ

A little more formal.

  1. Navaનવાnew
  2. varsh-niવર્ષનીof the year
  3. shubhkaamnaaoશુભકામનાઓgood wishes
