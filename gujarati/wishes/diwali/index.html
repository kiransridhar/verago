How to say “Happy Diwali” in Gujarati

Happy Diwali in Gujarati is Diwali-ni haardik shubhkaamnaao (દિવાળીની હાર્દિક શુભકામનાઓ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali is the festival of lights, in October or November. For Gujaratis the day after Diwali is also the new year, so two greetings are useful.

“Warm Diwali wishes”

Diwali-ni haardik shubhkaamnaaoદિવાળીની હાર્દિક શુભકામનાઓ

  1. Diwali-niદિવાળીનીof Diwali
  2. haardikહાર્દિકheartfelt
  3. shubhkaamnaaoશુભકામનાઓgood wishes

“Happy New Year”

Nutan varshaabhinandanનૂતન વર્ષાભિનંદન

Said on the Gujarati new year, the day after Diwali.

  1. Nutanનૂતનnew
  2. varshaabhinandanવર્ષાભિનંદનyear greetings

“Happy New Year”

Saal mubaarakસાલ મુબારક

A shorter new-year greeting.

  1. Saalસાલyear
  2. mubaarakમુબારકblessed, happy
