How to say “Happy Uttarayan” in Gujarati

Happy Uttarayan in Gujarati is Uttarayan-ni haardik shubhkaamnaao (ઉત્તરાયણની હાર્દિક શુભકામનાઓ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Uttarayan, on January 14, is Gujarat's kite festival. Families spend the day on the rooftop flying kites, and eat undhiyu and sesame chikki.

“Warm wishes for Uttarayan”
