Gujarati phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Gujarati

Kul ketlaa thayaa?કુલ કેટલા થયા?

  1. Kulકુલtotal
  2. ketlaaકેટલાhow much
  3. thayaa?થયા?became

How to say “I will pay by card” in Gujarati

Hu cardthi paisaa aapishહું કાર્ડથી પૈસા આપીશ

  1. HuહુંI
  2. cardthiકાર્ડથીby card
  3. paisaaપૈસાmoney
  4. aapishઆપીશwill give

How to say “Cash is fine” in Gujarati

Rokdaa paisaa chaalsheરોકડા પૈસા ચાલશે

  1. Rokdaaરોકડાcash
  2. paisaaપૈસાmoney
  3. chaalsheચાલશેwill do

How to say “Give me the change” in Gujarati

Chhuttaa paisaa aapoછુટ્ટા પૈસા આપો

  1. Chhuttaaછુટ્ટાloose
  2. paisaaપૈસાmoney
  3. aapoઆપોgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Gujarati

Aa bahu monghu chheઆ બહુ મોંઘું છે

  1. Aaઆthis
  2. bahuબહુvery
  3. monghuમોંઘુંexpensive
  4. chheછેis
