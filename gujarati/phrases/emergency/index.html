Gujarati phrases: Emergency!

Call for help when it counts. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Gujarati

Mehrbaani kari maari madad karoમહેરબાની કરી મારી મદદ કરો

  1. Mehrbaaniમહેરબાનીkindness
  2. kariકરીdoing
  3. maariમારીmy
  4. madadમદદhelp
  5. karoકરોdo

How to say “Call the police” in Gujarati

Policene phone karoપોલીસને ફોન કરો

  1. Policeneપોલીસનેto the police
  2. phoneફોનphone
  3. karoકરોdo

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Gujarati

Hospital kyaa chhe?હૉસ્પિટલ ક્યાં છે?

  1. Hospitalહૉસ્પિટલhospital
  2. kyaaક્યાંwhere
  3. chhe?છે?is

How to say “I am hurt” in Gujarati

Mane vaagyu chheમને વાગ્યું છે

  1. Maneમનેto me
  2. vaagyuવાગ્યુંgot hurt
  3. chheછેis

How to say “Please come quickly” in Gujarati

  1. Mehrbaaniમહેરબાનીkindness
  2. kariકરીdoing
  3. jaldiજલદીquickly
  4. aavoઆવોcome
