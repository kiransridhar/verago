Gujarati phrases: How I Feel

Talk about emotions. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I am happy today” in Gujarati

Aaje hu khush chhuઆજે હું ખુશ છું

  1. Aajeઆજેtoday
  2. huહુંI
  3. khushખુશhappy
  4. chhuછુંam

How to say “I am very tired” in Gujarati

Hu bahu thaaki gayo chhuહું બહુ થાકી ગયો છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. bahuબહુvery
  3. thaakiથાકીtired
  4. gayoગયોwent
  5. chhuછુંam

How to say “I am hungry” in Gujarati

Mane bhookh laagi chheમને ભૂખ લાગી છે

  1. Maneમનેto me
  2. bhookhભૂખhunger
  3. laagiલાગીstruck
  4. chheછેis
