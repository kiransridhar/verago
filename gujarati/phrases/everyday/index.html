Gujarati phrases: Everyday Sentences

Sentences for daily life. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I am going home” in Gujarati

Hu ghare jai rahyo chhuહું ઘરે જઈ રહ્યો છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. ghareઘરેhome
  3. jaiજઈgo
  4. rahyoરહ્યો-ing
  5. chhuછુંam

How to say “I am at school” in Gujarati

Hu schoolmaa chhuહું સ્કૂલમાં છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. schoolmaaસ્કૂલમાંin school
  3. chhuછુંam

How to say “It is hot today” in Gujarati

Aaje garmi chheઆજે ગરમી છે

  1. Aajeઆજેtoday
  2. garmiગરમીheat
  3. chheછેis
