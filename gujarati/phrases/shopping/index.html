Gujarati phrases: At the Shop

Sentences for shopping. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “How much is this?” in Gujarati

Aa ketlaanu chhe?આ કેટલાનું છે?

  1. Aaઆthis
  2. ketlaanuકેટલાનુંfor how much
  3. chhe?છે?is

How to say “Give me one mango” in Gujarati

Mane ek keri aapoમને એક કેરી આપો

  1. Maneમનેto me
  2. ekએકone
  3. keriકેરીmango
  4. aapoઆપોgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Gujarati

Aa bahu monghu chheઆ બહુ મોંઘું છે

  1. Aaઆthis
  2. bahuબહુvery
  3. monghuમોંઘુંexpensive
  4. chheછેis

How to say “Do you have rice?” in Gujarati

Shu tamaari paase chokhaa chhe?શું તમારી પાસે ચોખા છે?

  1. Shuશું(question)
  2. tamaariતમારીyour
  3. paaseપાસેnear
  4. chokhaaચોખાrice
  5. chhe?છે?is

How to say “I will take this” in Gujarati

Hu aa laishહું આ લઈશ

  1. HuહુંI
  2. aaઆthis
  3. laishલઈશwill take
