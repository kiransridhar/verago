Gujarati phrases: At the Office

Meetings and tasks at work. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I am busy now” in Gujarati

Hu atyaare vyast chhuહું અત્યારે વ્યસ્ત છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. atyaareઅત્યારેnow
  3. vyastવ્યસ્તbusy
  4. chhuછુંam

How to say “Please send the email” in Gujarati

Jaraa email mokaloજરા ઈમેલ મોકલો

  1. Jaraaજરાplease
  2. emailઈમેલemail
  3. mokaloમોકલોsend

How to say “The meeting is at 3” in Gujarati

Meeting tran vaage chheમીટિંગ ત્રણ વાગે છે

  1. Meetingમીટિંગmeeting
  2. tranત્રણthree
  3. vaageવાગેo'clock
  4. chheછેis

How to say “I have a lot of work” in Gujarati

Mare bahu kaam chheમારે બહુ કામ છે

  1. Mareમારેfor me
  2. bahuબહુa lot
  3. kaamકામwork
  4. chheછેis

How to say “See you tomorrow” in Gujarati

Kaale malishuકાલે મળીશું

  1. Kaaleકાલેtomorrow
  2. malishuમળીશુંwill meet
