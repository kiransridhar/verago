Gujarati phrases: Daily Routine

A day in the life. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I wake up at six” in Gujarati

Hu chha vaage uthu chhuહું છ વાગે ઊઠું છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. chhaછsix
  3. vaageવાગેo'clock
  4. uthuઊઠુંwake up
  5. chhuછુંam

How to say “I drink tea every morning” in Gujarati

Hu roj savaare chaa piu chhuહું રોજ સવારે ચા પીઉં છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. rojરોજevery day
  3. savaareસવારેin the morning
  4. chaaચાtea
  5. piuપીઉંdrink
  6. chhuછુંam

How to say “I go to work at nine” in Gujarati

  2. vaageવાગેo'clock
