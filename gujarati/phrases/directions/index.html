Gujarati phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “Where is the station?” in Gujarati

Station kyaa chhe?સ્ટેશન ક્યાં છે?

  1. Stationસ્ટેશનstation
  2. kyaaક્યાંwhere
  3. chhe?છે?is

How to say “Go straight ahead” in Gujarati

Seedhaa aagal jaaoસીધા આગળ જાઓ

  1. Seedhaaસીધાstraight
  2. aagalઆગળahead
  3. jaaoજાઓgo

How to say “Turn left there” in Gujarati

Tyaa daabi baaju valoત્યાં ડાબી બાજુ વળો

  1. Tyaaત્યાંthere
  2. daabiડાબીleft
  3. baajuબાજુside
  4. valoવળોturn

How to say “It is far from here” in Gujarati

Te ahithi door chheતે અહીંથી દૂર છે

  1. Teતેit
  2. ahithiઅહીંથીfrom here
  3. doorદૂરfar
  4. chheછેis

How to say “It is very close” in Gujarati

Te bahu najik chheતે બહુ નજીક છે

  1. Teતેit
  2. bahuબહુvery
  3. najikનજીકnear
  4. chheછેis
