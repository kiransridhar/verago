Gujarati phrases: Build Sentences

Tap words to form everyday sentences. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I eat rice” in Gujarati

Hu bhaat khaau chhuહું ભાત ખાઉં છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. bhaatભાતrice
  3. khaauખાઉંeat
  4. chhuછુંam

How to say “I drink water” in Gujarati

Hu paani piu chhuહું પાણી પીઉં છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. paaniપાણીwater
  3. piuપીઉંdrink
  4. chhuછુંam

How to say “I go to school” in Gujarati

Hu school jaau chhuહું સ્કૂલ જાઉં છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. schoolસ્કૂલto school
  3. jaauજાઉંgo
  4. chhuછુંam

How to say “My name is Ravi” in Gujarati

Maaru naam Ravi chheમારું નામ રવિ છે

  1. Maaruમારુંmy
  2. naamનામname
  3. RaviરવિRavi
  4. chheછેis

How to say “How are you” in Gujarati

Tame kem chhoતમે કેમ છો

  1. Tameતમેyou
  2. kemકેમhow
  3. chhoછોare
