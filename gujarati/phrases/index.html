Gujarati phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

GujaratiEnglish
🔁 Farithi boloફરીથી બોલો Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Dheeme dheeme boloધીમે ધીમે બોલો Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Maaf karjo, mane sambhalaayu nahiમાફ કરજો, મને સંભળાયું નહીં Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Mane samjaayu nahiમને સમજાયું નહીં I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Aa shu chhe?આ શું છે? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🧡 Aane Gujaratimaa shu kahevaay?આને ગુજરાતીમાં શું કહેવાય? What's this called in Gujarati?Ask for the Gujarati word when you only know the English.
📚 Hu Gujarati shikhu chhuહું ગુજરાતી શીખું છું I'm learning GujaratiSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Mane thodu Gujarati aavde chheમને થોડું ગુજરાતી આવડે છે I know a little GujaratiHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Tame lakhi aapsho?તમે લખી આપશો? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Angrejimaa kahesho?અંગ્રેજીમાં કહેશો? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
