Gujarati phrases: Weather Talk

Talk about the weather. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Gujarati

Aaje varsaad padi rahyo chheઆજે વરસાદ પડી રહ્યો છે

  1. Aajeઆજેtoday
  2. varsaadવરસાદrain
  3. padiપડીfall
  4. rahyoરહ્યો-ing
  5. chheછેis

How to say “The sun is hot” in Gujarati

Tadko bahu aakro chheતડકો બહુ આકરો છે

  1. Tadkoતડકોsunshine
  2. bahuબહુvery
  3. aakroઆકરોharsh
  4. chheછેis

How to say “It is cold here” in Gujarati

Ahi thandi chheઅહીં ઠંડી છે

  1. Ahiઅહીંhere
  2. thandiઠંડીcold
  3. chheછેis

How to say “There is a strong wind” in Gujarati

Jordaar pavan funkaay chheજોરદાર પવન ફૂંકાય છે

  1. Jordaarજોરદારstrong
  2. pavanપવનwind
  3. funkaayફૂંકાયblows
  4. chheછેis

How to say “The sky is blue” in Gujarati

Aakaash bhuru chheઆકાશ ભૂરું છે

  1. Aakaashઆકાશsky
  2. bhuruભૂરુંblue
  3. chheછેis
