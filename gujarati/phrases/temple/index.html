Gujarati phrases: At the Temple

Visit a temple respectfully. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I am going to the temple” in Gujarati

Hu mandire jai rahyo chhuહું મંદિરે જઈ રહ્યો છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. mandireમંદિરેto the temple
  3. jaiજઈgo
  4. rahyoરહ્યો-ing
  5. chhuછુંam

How to say “God bless you” in Gujarati

Bhagvaan tamaaru bhalu kareભગવાન તમારું ભલું કરે

  1. BhagvaanભગવાનGod
  2. tamaaruતમારુંyour
  3. bhaluભલુંgood
  4. kareકરેmay do

How to say “Take off your shoes” in Gujarati

Jodaa utaaroજોડા ઉતારો

  1. Jodaaજોડાshoes
  2. utaaroઉતારોtake off

How to say “It is a holy place” in Gujarati

Aa pavitra jagyaa chheઆ પવિત્ર જગ્યા છે

  1. Aaઆthis
  2. pavitraપવિત્રholy
  3. jagyaaજગ્યાplace
  4. chheછેis

How to say “I am praying” in Gujarati

Hu praarthanaa kari rahyo chhuહું પ્રાર્થના કરી રહ્યો છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. praarthanaaપ્રાર્થનાprayer
  3. kariકરીdo
  4. rahyoરહ્યો-ing
  5. chhuછુંam
