Gujarati phrases: At the Hotel

Check in and ask for help. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I want one room” in Gujarati

Mare ek room joie chheમારે એક રૂમ જોઈએ છે

  1. Mareમારેfor me
  2. ekએકone
  3. roomરૂમroom
  4. joieજોઈએwanted
  5. chheછેis

How to say “How much per night?” in Gujarati

Ek raatnaa ketlaa?એક રાતના કેટલા?

  1. Ekએકone
  2. raatnaaરાતનાof night
  3. ketlaa?કેટલા?how much

How to say “Where is my room?” in Gujarati

Maaro room kyaa chhe?મારો રૂમ ક્યાં છે?

  1. Maaroમારોmy
  2. roomરૂમroom
  3. kyaaક્યાંwhere
  4. chhe?છે?is

How to say “Bring breakfast please” in Gujarati

Jaraa naasto laavoજરા નાસ્તો લાવો

  1. Jaraaજરાplease
  2. naastoનાસ્તોbreakfast
  3. laavoલાવોbring

How to say “Wi-fi is not working” in Gujarati

Wi-fi chaaltu nathiવાઇ-ફાઇ ચાલતું નથી

  1. Wi-fiવાઇ-ફાઇwi-fi
  2. chaaltuચાલતુંrunning
  3. nathiનથીnot
