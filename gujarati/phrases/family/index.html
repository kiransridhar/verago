Gujarati phrases: Family Talk

Talk about family members. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “My mother is here” in Gujarati

Maaraa mummy ahi chheમારાં મમ્મી અહીં છે

  1. Maaraaમારાંmy
  2. mummyમમ્મીmother
  3. ahiઅહીંhere
  4. chheછેis

How to say “My father is at work” in Gujarati

  1. Maaraaમારાmy
  2. pappaપપ્પાfather
  3. kaameકામેat work
  4. chheછેis

How to say “I have one brother” in Gujarati

Mare ek bhai chheમારે એક ભાઈ છે

  1. Mareમારેfor me
  2. ekએકone
  3. bhaiભાઈbrother
  4. chheછેis

How to say “My sister is younger” in Gujarati

  1. Maariમારીmy
  2. benબેનsister

How to say “Grandmother tells stories” in Gujarati
