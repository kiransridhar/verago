Gujarati phrases: Introductions

Greet someone and introduce yourself. Here are 7 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “Hello, my name is Sita” in Gujarati

Namaste, maaru naam Sita chheનમસ્તે, મારું નામ સીતા છે

  1. Namaste,નમસ્તે,hello
  2. maaruમારુંmy
  3. naamનામname
  4. SitaસીતાSita
  5. chheછેis

How to say “I am from Vadodara” in Gujarati

Hu Vadodarano chhuહું વડોદરાનો છું

  1. HuહુંI
  2. Vadodaranoવડોદરાનોof Vadodara
  3. chhuછુંam

How to say “Do you speak Gujarati?” in Gujarati

Shu tame Gujarati bolo chho?શું તમે ગુજરાતી બોલો છો?

  1. Shuશું(question)
  2. tameતમેyou
  3. GujaratiગુજરાતીGujarati
  4. boloબોલોspeak
  5. chho?છો?are

How to say “See you tomorrow” in Gujarati

Kaale fari malishuકાલે ફરી મળીશું

  1. Kaaleકાલેtomorrow
  2. fariફરીagain
  3. malishuમળીશુંwill meet

How to say “What is your name?” in Gujarati

Tamaaru naam shu chhe?તમારું નામ શું છે?

  1. Tamaaruતમારુંyour
  2. naamનામname
  3. shuશુંwhat
  4. chhe?છે?is

How to say “Where are you from?” in Gujarati

Tame kyaanthi chho?તમે ક્યાંથી છો?

  1. Tameતમેyou
  2. kyaanthiક્યાંથીfrom where
  3. chho?છો?are

How to say “Nice to meet you” in Gujarati

Tamne maline aanand thayoતમને મળીને આનંદ થયો

  1. Tamneતમનેyou (to)
  2. malineમળીનેhaving met
  3. aanandઆનંદjoy
  4. thayoથયોhappened
