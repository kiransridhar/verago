Gujarati phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and pay the bill. Here are 5 Gujarati sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Gujarati says it.

How to say “I want one tea” in Gujarati

Mare ek chaa joie chheમારે એક ચા જોઈએ છે

  1. Mareમારેfor me
  2. ekએકone
  3. chaaચાtea
  4. joieજોઈએwanted
  5. chheછેis

How to say “The food is delicious” in Gujarati

Jamvaanu swaadisht chheજમવાનું સ્વાદિષ્ટ છે

  1. Jamvaanuજમવાનુંfood
  2. swaadishtસ્વાદિષ્ટtasty
  3. chheછેis

How to say “Bring water please” in Gujarati

Jaraa paani laavoજરા પાણી લાવો

  1. Jaraaજરાplease
  2. paaniપાણીwater
  3. laavoલાવોbring

How to say “Bill please” in Gujarati

Jaraa bill aapoજરા બિલ આપો

  1. Jaraaજરાplease
  2. billબિલbill
  3. aapoઆપોgive

How to say “No spicy food” in Gujarati

Teekhu jamvaanu nathi joituતીખું જમવાનું નથી જોઈતું

  1. Teekhuતીખુંspicy
  2. jamvaanuજમવાનુંfood
  3. nathiનથીnot
  4. joituજોઈતુંwanted
