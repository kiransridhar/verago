Learn Malayalam മലയാളം

Malayalam is the language of Kerala, spoken by about 35 million people in India and by large Malayali communities in the Gulf, the UK, the US and beyond. It has its own rounded script, related to Tamil.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Malayalam words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Malayalam script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
