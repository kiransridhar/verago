How to say “It’s okay” in Malayalam

“It’s okay” in Malayalam is Saaramilla (സാരമില്ല). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Saaramillaസാരമില്ല

Say it in 4 parts: Saa · ra · mi · lla

It is one of the 15 words in Malayalam polite phrases. The letters under the word are Malayalam script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
