How to say “Thank you” in Malayalam

“Thank you” in Malayalam is Nanni (നന്ദി). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Nanniനന്ദി

Say it in 2 parts: Na · nni

It is one of the 15 words in Malayalam greetings. The letters under the word are Malayalam script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
