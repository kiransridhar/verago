How to say “What are you doing?” in Malayalam

“What are you doing?” in Malayalam is Enthaa cheyyunne (എന്താ ചെയ്യുന്നേ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Enthaa cheyyunneഎന്താ ചെയ്യുന്നേ

It is one of the 15 words in Malayalam family phrases. The letters under the word are Malayalam script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
