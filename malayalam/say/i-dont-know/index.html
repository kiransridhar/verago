How to say “I don’t know” in Malayalam

“I don’t know” in Malayalam is Ariyilla (അറിയില്ല). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Ariyillaഅറിയില്ല

Say it in 4 parts: A · ri · yi · lla

It is one of the 15 words in Malayalam everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Malayalam script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
