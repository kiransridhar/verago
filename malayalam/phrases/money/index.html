Malayalam phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Malayalam sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Malayalam says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Malayalam

Mottham ethra aayiമൊത്തം എത്ര ആയി

  1. Motthamമൊത്തംtotal
  2. ethraഎത്രhow much
  3. aayiആയിbecame

How to say “I will pay by card” in Malayalam

Njaan cardil pay cheyyaamഞാൻ കാർഡിൽ പേ ചെയ്യാം

  1. NjaanഞാൻI
  2. cardilകാർഡിൽby card
  3. pay cheyyaamപേ ചെയ്യാംwill pay

How to say “Cash is fine” in Malayalam

Cash mathiക്യാഷ് മതി

  1. Cashക്യാഷ്cash
  2. mathiമതിis enough

How to say “Give me the change” in Malayalam

Baakki tharooബാക്കി തരൂ

  1. Baakkiബാക്കിchange
  2. tharooതരൂgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Malayalam

Ithinu valare vilayaanuഇതിന് വളരെ വിലയാണ്

  1. Ithinuഇതിന്for this
  2. valareവളരെvery
  3. vilayaanuവിലയാണ്is costly
