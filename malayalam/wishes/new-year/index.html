How to say “Happy New Year” in Malayalam

Happy New Year in Malayalam is Puthuvalsaraashamsakal (പുതുവത്സരാശംസകൾ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Puthuvalsaram means "new year" in Malayalam. The wish is used on January 1. Kerala also marks Vishu in the middle of April and the start of the month of Chingam in August as new beginnings.

“Happy New Year”

Puthuvalsaraashamsakalപുതുവത്സരാശംസകൾ

Puthu (new) + valsaram (year) + aashamsakal (wishes), written as one word.
