How to say “Happy Diwali” in Malayalam

Happy Diwali in Malayalam is Deepaavali aashamsakal (ദീപാവലി ആശംസകൾ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali, called Deepavali in Malayalam, is the festival of lights, in October or November. It is a smaller festival in Kerala than Onam or Vishu, but families light lamps and share sweets.
