How to say “Happy Makara Sankranti” in Malayalam

Happy Makara Sankranti in Malayalam is Makarasankraanthi aashamsakal (മകരസംക്രാന്തി ആശംസകൾ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Makara Sankranti comes in the middle of January. In Kerala the day is best known for Makaravilakku at Sabarimala. Kerala's own big festivals are Onam and Vishu, but this is the greeting to send friends and family who are celebrating.
