How to say “Happy Birthday” in Malayalam

Happy Birthday in Malayalam is Pirannaal aashamsakal (പിറന്നാൾ ആശംസകൾ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Malayalam has two everyday words for birthday: pirannaal and janmadinam.

“Happy birthday”

Pirannaal aashamsakalപിറന്നാൾ ആശംസകൾ

  1. Pirannaalപിറന്നാൾbirthday
  2. aashamsakalആശംസകൾwishes

“Happy birthday”

Janmadhinaashamsakalജന്മദിനാശംസകൾ

The same wish as a single word, common on cards.

  1. Janmadhinaashamsakalജന്മദിനാശംസകൾbirthday wishes (one word)
