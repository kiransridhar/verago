How to say “I don’t know” in Marathi

“I don’t know” in Marathi is Maahit naahii (माहित नाही). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Maahit naahiiमाहित नाही

It is one of the 15 words in Marathi everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Marathi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
