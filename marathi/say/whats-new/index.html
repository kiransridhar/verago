How to say “What’s new?” in Marathi

“What’s new?” in Marathi is Kaay vishesh? (काय विशेष?). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Kaay vishesh?काय विशेष?

It is one of the 15 words in Marathi family phrases. The letters under the word are Marathi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
