How to say “It’s okay” in Marathi

“It’s okay” in Marathi is Tiik aahe (ठीक आहे). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Tiik aaheठीक आहे

It is one of the 15 words in Marathi polite phrases. The letters under the word are Marathi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
