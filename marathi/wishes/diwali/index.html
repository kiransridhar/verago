How to say “Happy Diwali” in Marathi

Happy Diwali in Marathi is Divaaliichyaa haardik shubhechchhaa (दिवाळीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali is the festival of lights, in October or November. In Maharashtra families light lamps, hang a lantern (akash kandil) outside and make faral, the Diwali snacks and sweets.

“Warm Diwali wishes”

Divaaliichyaa haardik shubhechchhaaदिवाळीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा

  1. Divaaliichyaaदिवाळीच्याof Diwali
  2. haardikहार्दिकheartfelt
  3. shubhechchhaaशुभेच्छाgood wishes
