How to say “Happy Makar Sankranti” in Marathi

Happy Makar Sankranti in Marathi is Makar Sankraantiichyaa haardik shubhechchhaa (मकर संक्रांतीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Makar Sankranti comes on January 14 or 15, when the sun starts its journey north. In Maharashtra people give each other tilgul, small sweets of sesame and jaggery, and fly kites.

“Warm wishes for Makar Sankranti”

Makar Sankraantiichyaa haardik shubhechchhaaमकर संक्रांतीच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा

  1. MakarमकरMakar
  2. Sankraantiichyaaसंक्रांतीच्याof Sankranti
  3. haardikहार्दिकheartfelt
  4. shubhechchhaaशुभेच्छाgood wishes

“Take this tilgul and speak sweetly”

TiLguL ghyaa, goD goD bolaaतिळगुळ घ्या, गोड गोड बोला

Said while handing someone tilgul.

  1. TiLguLतिळगुळsesame-jaggery sweet
  2. ghyaaघ्याtake
  3. goDगोडsweet
  4. goDगोडsweet
  5. bolaaबोलाspeak
