How to say “Happy New Year” in Marathi

Happy New Year in Marathi is Naviin varshaachyaa haardik shubhechchhaa (नवीन वर्षाच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

This wish works on January 1 and also at Gudi Padwa, the Marathi new year, which comes in March or April.

“Warm wishes for the new year”

Naviin varshaachyaa haardik shubhechchhaaनवीन वर्षाच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा

  1. Naviinनवीनnew
  2. varshaachyaaवर्षाच्याof the year
  3. haardikहार्दिकheartfelt
  4. shubhechchhaaशुभेच्छाgood wishes
