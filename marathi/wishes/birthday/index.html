How to say “Happy Birthday” in Marathi

Happy Birthday in Marathi is VaaDhdivasaachyaa haardik shubhechchhaa (वाढदिवसाच्या हार्दिक शुभेच्छा). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

The Marathi word for birthday is vaadhdivas, the day you grow a year.

“Warm birthday wishes”
