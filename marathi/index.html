Learn Marathi मराठी

Marathi is the language of Maharashtra, spoken by more than 80 million people, from Mumbai and Pune to villages across the Deccan. It is written in Devanagari, the same script as Hindi.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Marathi words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Marathi script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
