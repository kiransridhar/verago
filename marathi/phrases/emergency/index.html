Marathi phrases: Emergency!

Call for help when it counts. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Marathi

Krupayaa madat karaaकृपया मदत करा

  1. Krupayaaकृपयाplease
  2. madatमदतhelp
  3. karaaकराdo

How to say “Call the police” in Marathi

Polisaannaa phon karaaपोलिसांना फोन करा

  1. Polisaannaaपोलिसांनाto the police
  2. phonफोनphone
  3. karaaकराdo

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Marathi

Rugnaalay kute aahe?रुग्णालय कुठे आहे?

  1. Rugnaalayरुग्णालयhospital
  2. kuteकुठेwhere
  3. aahe?आहे?is

How to say “I am hurt” in Marathi

  1. Malaaमलाto me
  2. laagaleलागलेhurt
  3. aaheआहेis
