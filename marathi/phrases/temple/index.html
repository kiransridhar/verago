How to say “I am going to the temple” in Marathi

Mii mandiraat jaatoमी मंदिरात जातो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. mandiraatमंदिरातto the temple
  3. jaatoजातोgo

How to say “God bless you” in Marathi

  1. Devaaneदेवानेby God
  2. krupaaकृपाgrace
  3. karaaviiकरावीmay do

How to say “It is a holy place” in Marathi

  3. sthaanस्थानplace
  4. aaheआहेis
