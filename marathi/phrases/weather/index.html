Marathi phrases: Weather Talk

Talk about the weather. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Marathi

Aaj paauus padatoyआज पाऊस पडतोय

  1. Aajआजtoday
  2. paauusपाऊसrain
  3. padatoyपडतोयis falling

How to say “The sun is hot” in Marathi

Uun khuup aaheऊन खूप आहे

  1. Uunऊनsunshine
  2. khuupखूपa lot
  3. aaheआहेis

How to say “It is cold here” in Marathi

Ithe thandii aaheइथे थंडी आहे

  1. Itheइथेhere
  2. thandiiथंडीcold
  3. aaheआहेis

How to say “There is a strong wind” in Marathi

Joraat vaaraa vaahatoyजोरात वारा वाहतोय

  1. Joraatजोरातstrongly
  2. vaaraaवाराwind
  3. vaahatoyवाहतोयis blowing

How to say “The sky is blue” in Marathi

Aakaash niLam aaheआकाश निळं आहे

  1. Aakaashआकाशsky
  2. niLamनिळंblue
  3. aaheआहेis
