How to say “I wake up at six” in Marathi

Mii sahaalaa uthatoमी सहाला उठतो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. sahaalaaसहालाat six
  3. uthatoउठतोwake up

How to say “I drink tea every morning” in Marathi

Mii roj sakaalii chahaa pitoमी रोज सकाळी चहा पितो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. rojरोजevery day
  3. sakaaliiसकाळीin the morning
  4. chahaaचहाtea
  5. pitoपितोdrink

How to say “I go to work at nine” in Marathi

Nauu vaajataa mii kaamaalaa jaatoनऊ वाजता मी कामाला जातो

  1. Nauuनऊnine
  2. vaajataaवाजताo'clock
  3. miiमीI
  4. kaamaalaaकामालाto work
  5. jaatoजातोgo

How to say “I eat dinner at eight” in Marathi

  2. vaajataaवाजताo'clock
  3. miiमीI
