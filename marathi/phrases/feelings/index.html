How to say “I am happy today” in Marathi

Aaj mii khush aaheआज मी खुश आहे

  1. Aajआजtoday
  2. miiमीI
  3. khushखुशhappy
  4. aaheआहेam

How to say “I am very tired” in Marathi

  1. MiiमीI
  2. khuupखूपvery
  3. thakaloथकलोtired
  4. aaheआहेam

How to say “I am hungry” in Marathi
