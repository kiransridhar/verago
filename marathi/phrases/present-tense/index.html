Marathi phrases: Build Sentences

Tap words to form everyday sentences. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “I eat rice” in Marathi

Mii bhaath khaathoमी भात खातो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. bhaathभातrice
  3. khaathoखातोeat

How to say “I drink water” in Marathi

Mii paanii pithoमी पाणी पितो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. paaniiपाणीwater
  3. pithoपितोdrink

How to say “I go to school” in Marathi

Mii shaaleth jaathoमी शाळेत जातो

  1. MiiमीI
  2. shaalethशाळेतto school
  3. jaathoजातोgo

How to say “My name is Ravi” in Marathi

Maajhe naav Ravii aaheमाझे नाव रवी आहे

  1. Maajheमाझेmy
  2. naavनावname
  3. RaviiरवीRavi
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “How are you” in Marathi

Thumhii kase aahaathतुम्ही कसे आहात

  1. Thumhiiतुम्हीyou
  2. kaseकसेhow
  3. aahaathआहातare
