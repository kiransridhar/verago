Marathi phrases: At the Office

Meetings and tasks at work. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “I am busy now” in Marathi

Mii aattaa vyasth aaheमी आत्ता व्यस्त आहे

  1. MiiमीI
  2. aattaaआत्ताnow
  3. vyasthव्यस्तbusy
  4. aaheआहेam

How to say “Please send the email” in Marathi

Krupayaa iimel paaThavaaकृपया ईमेल पाठवा

  1. Krupayaaकृपयाplease
  2. iimelईमेलemail
  3. paaThavaaपाठवाsend

How to say “The meeting is at 3” in Marathi

Miiting tiin vaajataa aaheमीटिंग तीन वाजता आहे

  1. Miitingमीटिंगmeeting
  2. tiinतीनthree
  3. vaajataaवाजताo'clock
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “I have a lot of work” in Marathi

Maajhyaakade khuup kaam aaheमाझ्याकडे खूप काम आहे

  1. Maajhyaakadeमाझ्याकडेwith me
  2. khuupखूपa lot
  3. kaamकामwork
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “See you tomorrow” in Marathi

  1. Udyaaउद्याtomorrow
  2. bhetuuभेटूlet's meet
