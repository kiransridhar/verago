Marathi phrases: Everyday Sentences

Sentences for daily life. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “I am going home” in Marathi

  1. MiiमीI
  2. ghariघरीhome
