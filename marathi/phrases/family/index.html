Marathi phrases: Family Talk

Talk about family members. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “My mother is here” in Marathi

Maajhii aaii ithe aaheमाझी आई इथे आहे

  1. Maajhiiमाझीmy
  2. aaiiआईmother
  3. itheइथेhere
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “My father is at work” in Marathi

Maajhe baabaa kaamaavar aahetमाझे बाबा कामावर आहेत

  1. Maajheमाझेmy
  2. baabaaबाबाfather
  3. kaamaavarकामावरat work
  4. aahetआहेतis

How to say “I have one brother” in Marathi

Malaa ek bhaauu aaheमला एक भाऊ आहे

  1. Malaaमलाto me
  2. ekएकone
  3. bhaauuभाऊbrother
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “My sister is younger” in Marathi

  1. Maajhiiमाझीmy
  2. bahiinबहीणsister
  3. lahaanलहानsmall
