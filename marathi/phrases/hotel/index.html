How to say “I want one room” in Marathi

Malaa ek kholii paahijeमला एक खोली पाहिजे

  1. Malaaमलाto me
  2. ekएकone
  3. kholiiखोलीroom
  4. paahijeपाहिजेwant

How to say “How much per night?” in Marathi

  2. raatricheरात्रीचेof night
  3. kitii?किती?how much

How to say “Where is my room?” in Marathi
