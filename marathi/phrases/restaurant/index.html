Order food and pay the bill. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “I want one tea” in Marathi

Malaa ek chahaa paahijeमला एक चहा पाहिजे

  1. Malaaमलाto me
  2. ekएकone
  3. chahaaचहाtea
  4. paahijeपाहिजेwant

How to say “The food is delicious” in Marathi

Jevan chavisht aaheजेवण चविष्ट आहे

  1. Jevanजेवणfood
  2. chavishtचविष्टtasty
  3. aaheआहेis

How to say “Bring water please” in Marathi

Krupayaa paanii aanaaकृपया पाणी आणा

  1. Krupayaaकृपयाplease
  2. paaniiपाणीwater
  3. aanaaआणाbring

How to say “Bill please” in Marathi

krupayaa Bil dyaकृपया बिल द्या

  1. krupayaaकृपयाplease
  2. Bilबिलbill
  3. dyaद्याgive

How to say “No spicy food” in Marathi

Thikhat jevan nakoतिखट जेवण नको

  1. Thikhatतिखटspicy
  2. jevanजेवणfood
  3. nakoनकोdon't want
