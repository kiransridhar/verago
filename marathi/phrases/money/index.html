Marathi phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Marathi

Ekuun kitii jhaale?एकूण किती झाले?

  1. Ekuunएकूणtotal
  2. kitiiकितीhow much
  3. jhaale?झाले?became

How to say “I will pay by card” in Marathi

Mii kaardane paise deiinमी कार्डने पैसे देईन

  1. MiiमीI
  2. kaardaneकार्डनेby card
  3. paiseपैसेmoney
  4. deiinदेईनwill give

How to say “Cash is fine” in Marathi

Rokh paise chaalelरोख पैसे चालेल

  1. Rokhरोखcash
  2. paiseपैसेmoney
  3. chaalelचालेलwill do

How to say “Give me the change” in Marathi

SuTTe paise dhyaaसुट्टे पैसे द्या

  1. SuTTeसुट्टेloose
  2. paiseपैसेmoney
  3. dhyaaद्याgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Marathi

He khuup mahaag aaheहे खूप महाग आहे

  1. Heहेthis
  2. khuupखूपvery
  3. mahaagमहागexpensive
  4. aaheआहेis
