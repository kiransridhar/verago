Marathi phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

MarathiEnglish
🔁 Punhaa saangaaपुन्हा सांगा Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Haluu haluu bolaaहळू हळू बोला Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Maaf karaa, malaa aikuu aala naahiiमाफ करा, मला ऐकू आलं नाही Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Malaa samajat naahiiमला समजत नाही I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ He kaay aahe?हे काय आहे? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🧡 Yaalaa Maraathiit kaay mhantaat?याला मराठीत काय म्हणतात? What's this called in Marathi?Ask for the Marathi word when you only know the English.
📚 Mii Maraathii shikat aaheमी मराठी शिकत आहे I'm learning MarathiSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Malaa thodii Maraathii yeteमला थोडी मराठी येते I know a little MarathiHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Lihuun deu shakataa kaa?लिहून देऊ शकता का? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Ingrajiit saangaal kaa?इंग्रजीत सांगाल का? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
