Marathi phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around. Here are 5 Marathi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Marathi says it.

How to say “Where is the station?” in Marathi

Sthaanak kute aahe?स्थानक कुठे आहे?

  1. Sthaanakस्थानकstation
  2. kuteकुठेwhere
  3. aahe?आहे?is

How to say “Go straight ahead” in Marathi

Saral puDhe jaaसरळ पुढे जा

  1. Saralसरळstraight
  2. puDheपुढेahead
  3. jaaजाgo

How to say “Turn left there” in Marathi

Tikade daaviikade vaLaaतिकडे डावीकडे वळा

  1. Tikadeतिकडेthere
  2. daaviikadeडावीकडेto the left
  3. vaLaaवळाturn

How to say “It is far from here” in Marathi

Te ithuun laamb aaheते इथून लांब आहे

  1. Teतेit
  2. ithuunइथूनfrom here
  3. laambलांबfar
  4. aaheआहेis

How to say “It is very close” in Marathi

Te khuup javaL aaheते खूप जवळ आहे

  1. Teतेit
  2. khuupखूपvery
  3. javaLजवळnear
  4. aaheआहेis
