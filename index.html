Learn them as an adventure

Verago is a free app that teaches Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam and Punjabi on an RPG journey: meet each word with a picture and hear it, speak your answers out loud, build your own village and battle bosses with what you've learned. No ads.
