How to say “Happy Diwali” in Punjabi

Happy Diwali in Punjabi is Diwali diyaan lakh lakh vadhaaiyaan (ਦੀਵਾਲੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali is the festival of lights, in October or November. Sikhs mark the same day as Bandi Chhor Divas, and gurdwaras and homes are lit with lamps.

“Many, many congratulations on Diwali”

Diwali diyaan lakh lakh vadhaaiyaanਦੀਵਾਲੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ

  1. DiwaliਦੀਵਾਲੀDiwali
  2. diyaanਦੀਆਂof
  3. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  4. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  5. vadhaaiyaanਵਧਾਈਆਂcongratulations

“Congratulations on Bandi Chhor Divas”

Bandi Chhor Divas diyaan vadhaaiyaanਬੰਦੀ ਛੋੜ ਦਿਵਸ ਦੀਆਂ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ

The Sikh greeting for the same day.

  1. Bandiਬੰਦੀprisoner
  2. Chhorਛੋੜrelease
  3. Divasਦਿਵਸday
  4. diyaanਦੀਆਂof
  5. vadhaaiyaanਵਧਾਈਆਂcongratulations
