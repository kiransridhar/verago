How to say “Happy Birthday” in Punjabi

Happy Birthday in Punjabi is Janamdin mubaarak (ਜਨਮਦਿਨ ਮੁਬਾਰਕ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

The Punjabi word for birthday is janamdin, "birth day".

“Happy birthday”

Janamdin mubaarakਜਨਮਦਿਨ ਮੁਬਾਰਕ

  1. Janamdinਜਨਮਦਿਨbirthday
  2. mubaarakਮੁਬਾਰਕblessed, happy

“Many, many congratulations on your birthday”

Janamdin diyaan lakh lakh vadhaaiyaanਜਨਮਦਿਨ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ

  1. Janamdinਜਨਮਦਿਨbirthday
  2. diyaanਦੀਆਂof
  3. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  4. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  5. vadhaaiyaanਵਧਾਈਆਂcongratulations
