How to say “Happy Lohri” in Punjabi

Happy Lohri in Punjabi is Lohri diyaan lakh lakh vadhaaiyaan (ਲੋਹੜੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Lohri is on January 13. Families gather around a bonfire in the evening, sing, and share sesame sweets, jaggery, peanuts and popcorn. The next day is Maghi.

“Many, many congratulations on Lohri”

Lohri diyaan lakh lakh vadhaaiyaanਲੋਹੜੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਲੱਖ ਲੱਖ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ

  1. LohriਲੋਹੜੀLohri
  2. diyaanਦੀਆਂof
  3. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  4. lakhਲੱਖa hundred thousand
  5. vadhaaiyaanਵਧਾਈਆਂcongratulations

“Congratulations on Maghi”

Maaghi diyaan vadhaaiyaanਮਾਘੀ ਦੀਆਂ ਵਧਾਈਆਂ

For Maghi, the day after Lohri.

  1. MaaghiਮਾਘੀMaghi
  2. diyaanਦੀਆਂof
  3. vadhaaiyaanਵਧਾਈਆਂcongratulations
