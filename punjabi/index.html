Learn Punjabi ਪੰਜਾਬੀ

Punjabi is the language of Punjab, spoken by more than 100 million people in India and Pakistan and by big communities in Canada, the UK and the US. In India it is written in Gurmukhi.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Punjabi words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Punjabi script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
