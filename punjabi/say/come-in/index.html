How to say “Come in” in Punjabi

“Come in” in Punjabi is Andar aao ji (ਅੰਦਰ ਆਓ ਜੀ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Andar aao jiਅੰਦਰ ਆਓ ਜੀ

It is one of the 15 words in Punjabi polite phrases. The letters under the word are Punjabi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
