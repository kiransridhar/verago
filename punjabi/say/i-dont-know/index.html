How to say “I don’t know” in Punjabi

“I don’t know” in Punjabi is Pata nahin (ਪਤਾ ਨਹੀਂ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Pata nahinਪਤਾ ਨਹੀਂ

It is one of the 15 words in Punjabi everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Punjabi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
