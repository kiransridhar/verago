How to say “What are you doing?” in Punjabi

“What are you doing?” in Punjabi is Ki kar rahe ho? (ਕੀ ਕਰ ਰਹੇ ਹੋ?). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Ki kar rahe ho?ਕੀ ਕਰ ਰਹੇ ਹੋ?

It is one of the 15 words in Punjabi family phrases. The letters under the word are Punjabi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
