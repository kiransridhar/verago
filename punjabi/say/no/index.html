How to say “No” in Punjabi

“No” in Punjabi is Nahin (ਨਹੀਂ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Nahinਨਹੀਂ

Say it in 2 parts: Na · hin

It is one of the 15 words in Punjabi greetings. The letters under the word are Punjabi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
