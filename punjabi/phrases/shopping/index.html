Punjabi phrases: At the Shop

Sentences for shopping. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “How much is this?” in Punjabi

Eh kinne da hai?ਇਹ ਕਿੰਨੇ ਦਾ ਹੈ?

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. kinneਕਿੰਨੇhow much
  3. daਦਾof
  4. hai?ਹੈ?is

How to say “Give me one mango” in Punjabi

Mainu ikk amb dioਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਅੰਬ ਦਿਓ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. ikkਇੱਕone
  3. ambਅੰਬmango
  4. dioਦਿਓgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Punjabi

Eh bahut mehnga haiਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗਾ ਹੈ

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. bahutਬਹੁਤvery
  3. mehngaਮਹਿੰਗਾexpensive
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “Do you have rice?” in Punjabi

Ki tuhade kol chaul han?ਕੀ ਤੁਹਾਡੇ ਕੋਲ ਚੌਲ ਹਨ?

  1. Kiਕੀ(question)
  2. tuhadeਤੁਹਾਡੇyour
  3. kolਕੋਲnear
  4. chaulਚੌਲrice
  5. han?ਹਨ?are

How to say “I will take this” in Punjabi

Main eh lavaangaਮੈਂ ਇਹ ਲਵਾਂਗਾ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. ehਇਹthis
  3. lavaangaਲਵਾਂਗਾwill take
