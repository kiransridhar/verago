Punjabi phrases: Weather Talk

Talk about the weather. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Punjabi

Ajj meenh pai reha haiਅੱਜ ਮੀਂਹ ਪੈ ਰਿਹਾ ਹੈ

  1. Ajjਅੱਜtoday
  2. meenhਮੀਂਹrain
  3. paiਪੈfall
  4. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
  5. haiਹੈis

How to say “The sun is hot” in Punjabi

Dhupp bahut tez haiਧੁੱਪ ਬਹੁਤ ਤੇਜ਼ ਹੈ

  1. Dhuppਧੁੱਪsunshine
  2. bahutਬਹੁਤvery
  3. tezਤੇਜ਼strong
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “It is cold here” in Punjabi

Itthe thand haiਇੱਥੇ ਠੰਡ ਹੈ

  1. Ittheਇੱਥੇhere
  2. thandਠੰਡcold
  3. haiਹੈis

How to say “There is a strong wind” in Punjabi

Tez hawa chall rahi haiਤੇਜ਼ ਹਵਾ ਚੱਲ ਰਹੀ ਹੈ

  1. Tezਤੇਜ਼strong
  2. hawaਹਵਾwind
  3. challਚੱਲmove
  4. rahiਰਹੀ-ing
  5. haiਹੈis

How to say “The sky is blue” in Punjabi

Asmaan neela haiਅਸਮਾਨ ਨੀਲਾ ਹੈ

  1. Asmaanਅਸਮਾਨsky
  2. neelaਨੀਲਾblue
  3. haiਹੈis
