Punjabi phrases: Everyday Sentences

Sentences for daily life. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I am going home” in Punjabi

Main ghar ja reha haanਮੈਂ ਘਰ ਜਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. gharਘਰhome
  3. jaਜਾgo
  4. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
  5. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “I am at school” in Punjabi

Main school vich haanਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. schoolਸਕੂਲschool
  3. vichਵਿੱਚin
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “It is hot today” in Punjabi

Ajj garmi haiਅੱਜ ਗਰਮੀ ਹੈ

  1. Ajjਅੱਜtoday
  2. garmiਗਰਮੀheat
  3. haiਹੈis

How to say “I am sleeping” in Punjabi

Main saun reha haanਮੈਂ ਸੌਂ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. saunਸੌਂsleep
  3. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “This book is mine” in Punjabi

Eh kitaab meri haiਇਹ ਕਿਤਾਬ ਮੇਰੀ ਹੈ

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. kitaabਕਿਤਾਬbook
  3. meriਮੇਰੀmine
  4. haiਹੈis
