Punjabi phrases: Emergency!

Call for help when it counts. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Punjabi

Meri madad karo jiਮੇਰੀ ਮਦਦ ਕਰੋ ਜੀ

  1. Meriਮੇਰੀmy
  2. madadਮਦਦhelp
  3. karoਕਰੋdo
  4. jiਜੀplease (respect)

How to say “Call the police” in Punjabi

Police nu phone karoਪੁਲਿਸ ਨੂੰ ਫ਼ੋਨ ਕਰੋ

  1. Policeਪੁਲਿਸpolice
  2. nuਨੂੰto
  3. phoneਫ਼ੋਨphone
  4. karoਕਰੋdo

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Punjabi

Hasptaal kitthe hai?ਹਸਪਤਾਲ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?

  1. Hasptaalਹਸਪਤਾਲhospital
  2. kittheਕਿੱਥੇwhere
  3. hai?ਹੈ?is

How to say “I am hurt” in Punjabi

Mainu satt laggi haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਸੱਟ ਲੱਗੀ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. sattਸੱਟinjury
  3. laggiਲੱਗੀstruck
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “Please come quickly” in Punjabi

Jaldi aao jiਜਲਦੀ ਆਓ ਜੀ

  1. Jaldiਜਲਦੀquickly
  2. aaoਆਓcome
  3. jiਜੀplease (respect)
