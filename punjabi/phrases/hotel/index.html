Punjabi phrases: At the Hotel

Check in and ask for help. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I want one room” in Punjabi

Mainu ikk kamra chaahida haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਕਮਰਾ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. ikkਇੱਕone
  3. kamraਕਮਰਾroom
  4. chaahidaਚਾਹੀਦਾwanted
  5. haiਹੈis

How to say “How much per night?” in Punjabi

Ikk raat da kinna?ਇੱਕ ਰਾਤ ਦਾ ਕਿੰਨਾ?

  1. Ikkਇੱਕone
  2. raatਰਾਤnight
  3. daਦਾof
  4. kinna?ਕਿੰਨਾ?how much

How to say “Where is my room?” in Punjabi

Mera kamra kitthe hai?ਮੇਰਾ ਕਮਰਾ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?

  1. Meraਮੇਰਾmy
  2. kamraਕਮਰਾroom
  3. kittheਕਿੱਥੇwhere
  4. hai?ਹੈ?is

How to say “Bring breakfast please” in Punjabi

Naashta le aao jiਨਾਸ਼ਤਾ ਲੈ ਆਓ ਜੀ

  1. Naashtaਨਾਸ਼ਤਾbreakfast
  2. leਲੈtake
  3. aaoਆਓcome
  4. jiਜੀplease (respect)

How to say “Wi-fi is not working” in Punjabi

Wifi nahin chall rehaਵਾਈਫ਼ਾਈ ਨਹੀਂ ਚੱਲ ਰਿਹਾ

  1. Wifiਵਾਈਫ਼ਾਈwi-fi
  2. nahinਨਹੀਂnot
  3. challਚੱਲrun
  4. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
