Punjabi phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

PunjabiEnglish
🔁 Dubaara bolo jiਦੁਬਾਰਾ ਬੋਲੋ ਜੀ Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Hauli hauli bolo jiਹੌਲੀ ਹੌਲੀ ਬੋਲੋ ਜੀ Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Maaf karna, mainu sunaai nahin dittaਮਾਫ਼ ਕਰਨਾ, ਮੈਨੂੰ ਸੁਣਾਈ ਨਹੀਂ ਦਿੱਤਾ Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Mainu samajh nahin aayiਮੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝ ਨਹੀਂ ਆਈ I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Eh ki hai?ਇਹ ਕੀ ਹੈ? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🧡 Isnu Punjabi vich ki kehnde ne?ਇਸਨੂੰ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਕੀ ਕਹਿੰਦੇ ਨੇ? What's this called in Punjabi?Ask for the Punjabi word when you only know the English.
📚 Main Punjabi sikkh reha haanਮੈਂ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਸਿੱਖ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ I'm learning PunjabiSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Mainu thorhi Punjabi aaundi haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਥੋੜ੍ਹੀ ਪੰਜਾਬੀ ਆਉਂਦੀ ਹੈ I know a little PunjabiHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Ki tusi likh ke de sakde ho?ਕੀ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਲਿਖ ਕੇ ਦੇ ਸਕਦੇ ਹੋ? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Angrezi vich dass sakde ho?ਅੰਗਰੇਜ਼ੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਦੱਸ ਸਕਦੇ ਹੋ? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
