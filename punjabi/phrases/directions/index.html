Punjabi phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “Where is the station?” in Punjabi

Station kitthe hai?ਸਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਕਿੱਥੇ ਹੈ?

  1. Stationਸਟੇਸ਼ਨstation
  2. kittheਕਿੱਥੇwhere
  3. hai?ਹੈ?is

How to say “Go straight ahead” in Punjabi

Siddhe agge jaoਸਿੱਧੇ ਅੱਗੇ ਜਾਓ

  1. Siddheਸਿੱਧੇstraight
  2. aggeਅੱਗੇahead
  3. jaoਜਾਓgo

How to say “Turn left there” in Punjabi

Utthe khabbe muroਉੱਥੇ ਖੱਬੇ ਮੁੜੋ

  1. Uttheਉੱਥੇthere
  2. khabbeਖੱਬੇto the left
  3. muroਮੁੜੋturn

How to say “It is far from here” in Punjabi

Uh itthon door haiਉਹ ਇੱਥੋਂ ਦੂਰ ਹੈ

  1. Uhਉਹit
  2. itthonਇੱਥੋਂfrom here
  3. doorਦੂਰfar
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “It is very close” in Punjabi

Uh bahut nere haiਉਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਨੇੜੇ ਹੈ

  1. Uhਉਹit
  2. bahutਬਹੁਤvery
  3. nereਨੇੜੇnear
  4. haiਹੈis
