Punjabi phrases: At the Gurdwara

Visit a gurdwara respectfully. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I am going to the gurdwara” in Punjabi

Main gurdware ja reha haanਮੈਂ ਗੁਰਦੁਆਰੇ ਜਾ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. gurdwareਗੁਰਦੁਆਰੇto the gurdwara
  3. jaਜਾgo
  4. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
  5. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “God bless you” in Punjabi

Rabb tuhada bhala kareਰੱਬ ਤੁਹਾਡਾ ਭਲਾ ਕਰੇ

  1. RabbਰੱਬGod
  2. tuhadaਤੁਹਾਡਾyour
  3. bhalaਭਲਾgood
  4. kareਕਰੇmay do

How to say “Take off your shoes” in Punjabi

Jutte utaaroਜੁੱਤੇ ਉਤਾਰੋ

  1. Jutteਜੁੱਤੇshoes
  2. utaaroਉਤਾਰੋtake off

How to say “It is a holy place” in Punjabi

Eh pavittar jagah haiਇਹ ਪਵਿੱਤਰ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਹੈ

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. pavittarਪਵਿੱਤਰholy
  3. jagahਜਗ੍ਹਾplace
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “I am praying” in Punjabi

Main ardaas kar reha haanਮੈਂ ਅਰਦਾਸ ਕਰ ਰਿਹਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. ardaasਅਰਦਾਸprayer
  3. karਕਰdo
  4. rehaਰਿਹਾ-ing
  5. haanਹਾਂam
