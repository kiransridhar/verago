Punjabi phrases: At the Office

Meetings and tasks at work. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I am busy now” in Punjabi

Main hun busy haanਮੈਂ ਹੁਣ ਬਿਜ਼ੀ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. hunਹੁਣnow
  3. busyਬਿਜ਼ੀbusy
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “Please send the email” in Punjabi

Email bhejo jiਈਮੇਲ ਭੇਜੋ ਜੀ

  1. Emailਈਮੇਲemail
  2. bhejoਭੇਜੋsend
  3. jiਜੀplease (respect)

How to say “The meeting is at 3” in Punjabi

Meeting tinn vaje haiਮੀਟਿੰਗ ਤਿੰਨ ਵਜੇ ਹੈ

  1. Meetingਮੀਟਿੰਗmeeting
  2. tinnਤਿੰਨthree
  3. vajeਵਜੇo'clock
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “I have a lot of work” in Punjabi

Mere kol bahut kamm haiਮੇਰੇ ਕੋਲ ਬਹੁਤ ਕੰਮ ਹੈ

  1. Mereਮੇਰੇmy
  2. kolਕੋਲnear
  3. bahutਬਹੁਤa lot
  4. kammਕੰਮwork
  5. haiਹੈis

How to say “See you tomorrow” in Punjabi

Kall milaangeਕੱਲ੍ਹ ਮਿਲਾਂਗੇ

  1. Kallਕੱਲ੍ਹtomorrow
  2. milaangeਮਿਲਾਂਗੇwill meet
