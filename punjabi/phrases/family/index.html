Punjabi phrases: Family Talk

Talk about family members. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “My mother is here” in Punjabi

Meri maa itthe hanਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਂ ਇੱਥੇ ਹਨ

  1. Meriਮੇਰੀmy
  2. maaਮਾਂmother
  3. ittheਇੱਥੇhere
  4. hanਹਨis (respect)

How to say “My father is at work” in Punjabi

Mere papa kamm te hanਮੇਰੇ ਪਾਪਾ ਕੰਮ ਤੇ ਹਨ

  1. Mereਮੇਰੇmy
  2. papaਪਾਪਾfather
  3. kammਕੰਮwork
  4. teਤੇat
  5. hanਹਨis (respect)

How to say “I have one brother” in Punjabi

Mera ikk bhra haiਮੇਰਾ ਇੱਕ ਭਰਾ ਹੈ

  1. Meraਮੇਰਾmy
  2. ikkਇੱਕone
  3. bhraਭਰਾbrother
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “My sister is younger” in Punjabi

Meri bhain chhoti haiਮੇਰੀ ਭੈਣ ਛੋਟੀ ਹੈ

  1. Meriਮੇਰੀmy
  2. bhainਭੈਣsister
  3. chhotiਛੋਟੀsmall
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “Grandmother tells stories” in Punjabi

Biji kahaaniaan sunaunde hanਬੀਜੀ ਕਹਾਣੀਆਂ ਸੁਣਾਉਂਦੇ ਹਨ

  1. Bijiਬੀਜੀgrandmother
  2. kahaaniaanਕਹਾਣੀਆਂstories
  3. sunaundeਸੁਣਾਉਂਦੇtell
  4. hanਹਨare (respect)
