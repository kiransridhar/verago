Punjabi phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and pay the bill. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I want one tea” in Punjabi

Mainu ikk chaah chaahidi haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਇੱਕ ਚਾਹ ਚਾਹੀਦੀ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. ikkਇੱਕone
  3. chaahਚਾਹtea
  4. chaahidiਚਾਹੀਦੀwanted
  5. haiਹੈis

How to say “The food is delicious” in Punjabi

Khaana suaadi haiਖਾਣਾ ਸੁਆਦੀ ਹੈ

  1. Khaanaਖਾਣਾfood
  2. suaadiਸੁਆਦੀtasty
  3. haiਹੈis

How to say “Bring water please” in Punjabi

Paani le aao jiਪਾਣੀ ਲੈ ਆਓ ਜੀ

  1. Paaniਪਾਣੀwater
  2. leਲੈtake
  3. aaoਆਓcome
  4. jiਜੀplease (respect)

How to say “Bill please” in Punjabi

Bill dio jiਬਿੱਲ ਦਿਓ ਜੀ

  1. Billਬਿੱਲbill
  2. dioਦਿਓgive
  3. jiਜੀplease (respect)

How to say “No spicy food” in Punjabi

Tikkha khaana nahin chaahidaਤਿੱਖਾ ਖਾਣਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਚਾਹੀਦਾ

  1. Tikkhaਤਿੱਖਾspicy
  2. khaanaਖਾਣਾfood
  3. nahinਨਹੀਂnot
  4. chaahidaਚਾਹੀਦਾwanted
