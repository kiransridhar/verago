Punjabi phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Punjabi

Kull kinna hoya?ਕੁੱਲ ਕਿੰਨਾ ਹੋਇਆ?

  1. Kullਕੁੱਲtotal
  2. kinnaਕਿੰਨਾhow much
  3. hoya?ਹੋਇਆ?became

How to say “I will pay by card” in Punjabi

Main card naal paise devaangaਮੈਂ ਕਾਰਡ ਨਾਲ ਪੈਸੇ ਦੇਵਾਂਗਾ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. cardਕਾਰਡcard
  3. naalਨਾਲwith
  4. paiseਪੈਸੇmoney
  5. devaangaਦੇਵਾਂਗਾwill give

How to say “Cash is fine” in Punjabi

Nakad paise chall jaangeਨਕਦ ਪੈਸੇ ਚੱਲ ਜਾਣਗੇ

  1. Nakadਨਕਦcash
  2. paiseਪੈਸੇmoney
  3. challਚੱਲrun
  4. jaangeਜਾਣਗੇwill (go)

How to say “Give me the change” in Punjabi

Khulle paise dioਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੇ ਪੈਸੇ ਦਿਓ

  1. Khulleਖੁੱਲ੍ਹੇloose
  2. paiseਪੈਸੇmoney
  3. dioਦਿਓgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Punjabi

Eh bahut mehnga haiਇਹ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਹਿੰਗਾ ਹੈ

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. bahutਬਹੁਤvery
  3. mehngaਮਹਿੰਗਾexpensive
  4. haiਹੈis
