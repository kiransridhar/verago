Punjabi phrases: Ask Questions

Build question sentences. Here are 6 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “Who are you?” in Punjabi

Tusi kaun ho?ਤੁਸੀਂ ਕੌਣ ਹੋ?

  1. Tusiਤੁਸੀਂyou
  2. kaunਕੌਣwho
  3. ho?ਹੋ?are

How to say “What is this?” in Punjabi

Eh ki hai?ਇਹ ਕੀ ਹੈ?

  1. Ehਇਹthis
  2. kiਕੀwhat
  3. hai?ਹੈ?is
