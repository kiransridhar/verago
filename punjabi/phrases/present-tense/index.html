Punjabi phrases: Build Sentences

Tap words to form everyday sentences. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I eat rice” in Punjabi

Main chaul khaanda haanਮੈਂ ਚੌਲ ਖਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. chaulਚੌਲrice
  3. khaandaਖਾਂਦਾeat
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “I drink water” in Punjabi

Main paani peenda haanਮੈਂ ਪਾਣੀ ਪੀਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. paaniਪਾਣੀwater
  3. peendaਪੀਂਦਾdrink
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “I go to school” in Punjabi

Main school jaanda haanਮੈਂ ਸਕੂਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. schoolਸਕੂਲto school
  3. jaandaਜਾਂਦਾgo
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “My name is Ravi” in Punjabi

Mera naam Ravi haiਮੇਰਾ ਨਾਮ ਰਵੀ ਹੈ

  1. Meraਮੇਰਾmy
  2. naamਨਾਮname
  3. RaviਰਵੀRavi
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “How are you” in Punjabi

Tusi kiven hoਤੁਸੀਂ ਕਿਵੇਂ ਹੋ

  1. Tusiਤੁਸੀਂyou
  2. kivenਕਿਵੇਂhow
  3. hoਹੋare
