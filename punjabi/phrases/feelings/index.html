Punjabi phrases: How I Feel

Talk about emotions. Here are 5 Punjabi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Punjabi says it.

How to say “I am happy today” in Punjabi

Ajj main khush haanਅੱਜ ਮੈਂ ਖੁਸ਼ ਹਾਂ

  1. Ajjਅੱਜtoday
  2. mainਮੈਂI
  3. khushਖੁਸ਼happy
  4. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “I am very tired” in Punjabi

Main bahut thakk gaya haanਮੈਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਥੱਕ ਗਿਆ ਹਾਂ

  1. MainਮੈਂI
  2. bahutਬਹੁਤvery
  3. thakkਥੱਕtired
  4. gayaਗਿਆgot
  5. haanਹਾਂam

How to say “I am hungry” in Punjabi

Mainu bhukh laggi haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਭੁੱਖ ਲੱਗੀ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. bhukhਭੁੱਖhunger
  3. laggiਲੱਗੀstruck
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “I am scared” in Punjabi

Mainu darr lagda haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਡਰ ਲੱਗਦਾ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. darrਡਰfear
  3. lagdaਲੱਗਦਾfeels
  4. haiਹੈis

How to say “I love this” in Punjabi

Mainu eh pasand haiਮੈਨੂੰ ਇਹ ਪਸੰਦ ਹੈ

  1. Mainuਮੈਨੂੰto me
  2. ehਇਹthis
  3. pasandਪਸੰਦliked
  4. haiਹੈis
