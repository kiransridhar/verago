The Punjabi alphabet ਪੰਜਾਬੀ

50 Punjabi letters in 8 groups. Each letter is shown with the sound it makes, written in English letters, and a word that uses it. Tap 🔊 to hear the letter.

Gurmukhi opens with three vowel carriers (ੳ ਅ ੲ). With vowel signs they make the ten vowels of Punjabi.

Velar consonants (throat) Kavarga

The "k" row — made in the back of the throat. In Punjabi ਘ is said with a falling tone.

LetterSoundExample word
ਕ ka kabootarਕਬੂਤਰPigeon
ਖ kha khargoshਖਰਗੋਸ਼Rabbit
ਗ ga gaanਗਾਂCow
ਘ gha gharਘਰHouse
ਙ nga (rare)ਙNasal — rarely starts a word

Palatal consonants Chavarga

The "ch" row — made with the tongue against the palate. ਝ carries a falling tone.

LetterSoundExample word
ਚ cha chaahਚਾਹTea
ਛ chha chhatriਛਤਰੀUmbrella
ਜ ja juttiਜੁੱਤੀShoe
ਝ jha jhandaਝੰਡਾFlag
ਞ nya (rare)ਞNasal — rarely starts a word

Retroflex consonants Tavarga

The hard "T" row — tongue curled back. ਣ is common inside Punjabi words.

ਣ ṇa paaniਪਾਣੀWater

A dot at the foot of a letter (pairin bindi) gives sounds borrowed from Persian, Arabic and English.
