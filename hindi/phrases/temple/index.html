Hindi phrases: At the Temple

Visit a temple respectfully. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “I am going to the temple” in Hindi

Main mandir jaa raha hoonमैं मंदिर जा रहा हूँ

  1. MainमैंI
  2. mandirमंदिरto the temple
  3. jaaजाgo
  4. rahaरहा-ing
  5. hoonहूँam

How to say “God bless you” in Hindi

  1. BhagwaanभगवानGod
  2. aapkaआपकाyour
  3. bhalaभलाgood
  4. kareकरेmay do

How to say “Take off your shoes” in Hindi

  1. Jooteजूतेshoes
  2. utaariyeउतारिएtake off
