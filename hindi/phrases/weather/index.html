Hindi phrases: Weather Talk

Talk about the weather. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Hindi

Aaj baarish ho rahi haiआज बारिश हो रही है

  1. Aajआजtoday
  2. baarishबारिशrain
  3. hoहोhappen
  4. rahiरही-ing
  5. haiहैis
