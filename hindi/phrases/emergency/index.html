Hindi phrases: Emergency!

Call for help when it counts. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Hindi

Kripya meri madad kijiyeकृपया मेरी मदद कीजिए

  1. Kripyaकृपयाplease
  2. meriमेरीmy
  3. madadमददhelp
  4. kijiyeकीजिएdo

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Hindi
