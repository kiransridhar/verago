Hindi phrases: Everyday Sentences

Sentences for daily life. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “I am going home” in Hindi

Main ghar jaa raha hoonमैं घर जा रहा हूँ

  1. MainमैंI
  2. gharघरhome
  3. jaaजाgo
  4. rahaरहा-ing
  5. hoonहूँam
