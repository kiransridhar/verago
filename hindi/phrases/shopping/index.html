How to say “How much is this?” in Hindi

Yeh kitne ka hai?यह कितने का है?

  1. Yehयहthis
  2. kitneकितनेhow much
  3. kaकाof
  4. hai?है?is

How to say “Give me one mango” in Hindi

  1. Mujheमुझेto me
  2. ekएकone
  3. aamआमmango
  4. dijiyeदीजिएgive
