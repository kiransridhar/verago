Hindi phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Hindi

Kul kitna hua?कुल कितना हुआ?

  1. Kulकुलtotal
  2. kitnaकितनाhow much
  3. hua?हुआ?became

How to say “I will pay by card” in Hindi

Main card se paise doongaमैं कार्ड से पैसे दूँगा

  1. MainमैंI
  2. cardकार्डcard
  3. seसेby
  4. paiseपैसेmoney
  5. doongaदूँगाwill give

How to say “Cash is fine” in Hindi

Nakad paise chalengeनकद पैसे चलेंगे

  1. Nakadनकदcash
  2. paiseपैसेmoney
  3. chalengeचलेंगेwill do

How to say “Give me the change” in Hindi

Khule paise dijiyeखुले पैसे दीजिए

  1. Khuleखुलेloose
  2. paiseपैसेmoney
  3. dijiyeदीजिएgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Hindi

Yeh bahut mehnga haiयह बहुत महँगा है

  1. Yehयहthis
  2. bahutबहुतvery
  3. mehngaमहँगाexpensive
  4. haiहैis
