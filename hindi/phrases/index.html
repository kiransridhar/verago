Hindi phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

HindiEnglish
🔁 Phir se boliyeफिर से बोलिए Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Dheere dheere boliyeधीरे धीरे बोलिए Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Maaf kijiye, mujhe sunaai nahin diyaमाफ़ कीजिए, मुझे सुनाई नहीं दिया Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Mujhe samajh nahin aayaमुझे समझ नहीं आया I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Yeh kya hai?यह क्या है? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🧡 Isko Hindi mein kya kehte hain?इसको हिंदी में क्या कहते हैं? What's this called in Hindi?Ask for the Hindi word when you only know the English.
📚 Main Hindi seekh raha hoonमैं हिंदी सीख रहा हूँ I'm learning HindiSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Mujhe thodi Hindi aati haiमुझे थोड़ी हिंदी आती है I know a little HindiHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Kya aap likhkar de sakte hain?क्या आप लिखकर दे सकते हैं? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Angrezi mein bataayenge?अंग्रेज़ी में बताएँगे? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
