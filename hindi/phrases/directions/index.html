Hindi phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “Where is the station?” in Hindi

Station kahaan hai?स्टेशन कहाँ है?

  1. Stationस्टेशनstation
  2. kahaanकहाँwhere
  3. hai?है?is

How to say “Go straight ahead” in Hindi

Seedhe aage jaaiyeसीधे आगे जाइए

  1. Seedheसीधेstraight
  2. aageआगेahead
  3. jaaiyeजाइएgo

How to say “Turn left there” in Hindi

Wahaan baayen mudiyeवहाँ बाएँ मुड़िए

  1. Wahaanवहाँthere
  2. baayenबाएँto the left
  3. mudiyeमुड़िएturn

How to say “It is far from here” in Hindi

Woh yahaan se door haiवह यहाँ से दूर है

  1. Wohवहit
  2. yahaanयहाँhere
  3. seसेfrom
  4. doorदूरfar
  5. haiहैis

How to say “It is very close” in Hindi

Woh bahut paas haiवह बहुत पास है

  1. Wohवहit
  2. bahutबहुतvery
  3. paasपासnear
  4. haiहैis
