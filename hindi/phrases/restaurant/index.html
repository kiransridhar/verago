Hindi phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and pay the bill. Here are 5 Hindi sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Hindi says it.

How to say “I want one tea” in Hindi

Mujhe ek chai chahiyeमुझे एक चाय चाहिए

  1. Mujheमुझेto me
  2. ekएकone
  3. chaiचायtea
  4. chahiyeचाहिएwant

How to say “The food is delicious” in Hindi

Khaana swaadisht haiखाना स्वादिष्ट है

  1. Khaanaखानाfood
  2. swaadishtस्वादिष्टtasty
  3. haiहैis

How to say “Bring water please” in Hindi

Kripya paani laaiyeकृपया पानी लाइए

  1. Kripyaकृपयाplease
  2. paaniपानीwater
  3. laaiyeलाइएbring

How to say “Bill please” in Hindi

Kripya bill dijiyeकृपया बिल दीजिए

  1. Kripyaकृपयाplease
  2. billबिलbill
  3. dijiyeदीजिएgive

How to say “No spicy food” in Hindi

  1. Teekhaतीखाspicy
  2. khaanaखानाfood
  3. nahinनहींnot
