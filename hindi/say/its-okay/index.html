How to say “It’s okay” in Hindi

“It’s okay” in Hindi is Theek hai (ठीक है). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Theek haiठीक है

It is one of the 15 words in Hindi polite phrases. The letters under the word are Hindi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
