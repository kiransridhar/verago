How to say “I don’t know” in Hindi

“I don’t know” in Hindi is Pata nahin (पता नहीं). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Pata nahinपता नहीं

It is one of the 15 words in Hindi everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Hindi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
