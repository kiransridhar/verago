How to say “Sorry” in Hindi

“Sorry” in Hindi is Maaf kijiye (माफ़ कीजिए). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Maaf kijiyeमाफ़ कीजिए

It is one of the 15 words in Hindi greetings. The letters under the word are Hindi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
