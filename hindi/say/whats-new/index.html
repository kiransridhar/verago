How to say “What’s new?” in Hindi

“What’s new?” in Hindi is Kya chal raha hai? (क्या चल रहा है?). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Kya chal raha hai?क्या चल रहा है?

It is one of the 15 words in Hindi family phrases. The letters under the word are Hindi script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
