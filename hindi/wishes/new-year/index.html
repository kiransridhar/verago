How to say “Happy New Year” in Hindi

Happy New Year in Hindi is Naya saal mubaarak (नया साल मुबारक). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Hindi has two everyday words for year: saal and the more formal varsh. Both wishes below are used on January 1.

“Happy New Year”

Naya saal mubaarakनया साल मुबारक

  1. Nayaनयाnew
  2. saalसालyear
  3. mubaarakमुबारकblessed, happy

“Good wishes for the new year”

Nav varsh ki shubhkaamnaayenनव वर्ष की शुभकामनाएँ

A little more formal.

  1. Navनवnew
  2. varshवर्षyear
  3. kiकीof
  4. shubhkaamnaayenशुभकामनाएँgood wishes
