How to say “Happy Makar Sankranti” in Hindi

Happy Makar Sankranti in Hindi is Makar Sankranti ki haardik shubhkaamnaayen (मकर संक्रांति की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Makar Sankranti comes on January 14 or 15, when the sun starts its journey north. Across north India people fly kites and eat sweets made of sesame and jaggery, and in many homes khichdi.
