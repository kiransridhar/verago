How to say “Happy Diwali” in Hindi

Happy Diwali in Hindi is Diwali ki haardik shubhkaamnaayen (दीवाली की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali is the festival of lights, in October or November. Homes are lit with rows of oil lamps (diye), families do Lakshmi puja and share sweets.
