How to say “Happy Sankranti” in Kannada

Happy Sankranti in Kannada is Sankraanthi habbadha shubhaashayagalu (ಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ ಹಬ್ಬದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Sankranti (Makara Sankranti) comes on January 14 or 15, when the sun starts its journey north. In Karnataka families give each other ellu-bella, a mix of sesame, jaggery, coconut and peanuts, along with pieces of sugarcane.

“Happy Sankranti”

Sankraanthi habbadha shubhaashayagaluಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿ ಹಬ್ಬದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು

  1. Sankraanthiಸಂಕ್ರಾಂತಿSankranti
  2. habbadhaಹಬ್ಬದof the festival
  3. shubhaashayagaluಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳುgood wishes

“Eat sesame and jaggery, and speak good words”

Ellu bella thindhu olle maathaadiಎಳ್ಳು ಬೆಲ್ಲ ತಿಂದು ಒಳ್ಳೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿ

The saying that goes with handing over ellu-bella.

  1. Elluಎಳ್ಳುsesame
  2. bellaಬೆಲ್ಲjaggery
  3. thindhuತಿಂದುhaving eaten
  4. olleಒಳ್ಳೆgood
  5. maathaadiಮಾತಾಡಿspeak
