How to say “Happy New Year” in Kannada

Happy New Year in Kannada is Hosa varshadha shubhaashayagalu (ಹೊಸ ವರ್ಷದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

This wish works on January 1 and also at Ugadi, the Kannada new year, which comes in March or April. For Ugadi people usually say "Yugaadi habbadha shubhaashayagalu", good wishes for the Ugadi festival.

“Happy New Year”

Hosa varshadha shubhaashayagaluಹೊಸ ವರ್ಷದ ಶುಭಾಶಯಗಳು

  1. Hosaಹೊಸnew
  2. varshadhaವರ್ಷದof the year
