Kannada phrases: Feelings & Emotions

Express how you feel. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I am happy” in Kannada

Naanu santhooshavaagidhdheeneನಾನು ಸಂತೋಷವಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. santhooshavaagidhdheeneಸಂತೋಷವಾಗಿದ್ದೇನೆam happy

How to say “I am tired” in Kannada

Nanage susthu aagidheನನಗೆ ಸುಸ್ತು ಆಗಿದೆ

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. susthuಸುಸ್ತುtiredness
  3. aagidheಆಗಿದೆhas become
