Kannada phrases: Money & ATM

Handle cash, cards, and the ATM. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “Where is the ATM?” in Kannada

ATM elli idheಎಟಿಎಂ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ?

  1. ATMಎಟಿಎಂATM
  2. elliಎಲ್ಲಿwhere
  3. idheಇದೆ?is

How to say “I need cash” in Kannada

Nanage nagadhu beekuನನಗೆ ನಗದು ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. nagadhuನಗದುcash
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “Do you take card?” in Kannada

Niivu kaard thagoththiiraaನೀವು ಕಾರ್ಡ್ ತಗೊತ್ತೀರಾ?

  1. Niivuನೀವುyou
  2. kaardಕಾರ್ಡ್card
  3. thagoththiiraaತಗೊತ್ತೀರಾ?take?

How to say “Cash only” in Kannada

Keevala nagadhu maathraಕೇವಲ ನಗದು ಮಾತ್ರ

  1. Keevalaಕೇವಲmerely
  2. nagadhuನಗದುcash
  3. maathraಮಾತ್ರonly

How to say “Give me change” in Kannada

Chillare kodiಚಿಲ್ಲರೆ ಕೊಡಿ

  1. Chillareಚಿಲ್ಲರೆchange
  2. kodiಕೊಡಿgive

How to say “This is too expensive” in Kannada

Idhu thumbaa dhubaariಇದು ತುಂಬಾ ದುಬಾರಿ

  1. Idhuಇದುthis
  2. thumbaaತುಂಬಾvery
  3. dhubaariದುಬಾರಿexpensive

How to say “Can you reduce the price?” in Kannada

Bele kammi maadiಬೆಲೆ ಕಮ್ಮಿ ಮಾಡಿ

  1. Beleಬೆಲೆprice
  2. kammiಕಮ್ಮಿless
  3. maadiಮಾಡಿmake
