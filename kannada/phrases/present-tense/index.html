Kannada phrases: Build Sentences

Tap words to form sentences. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I eat” in Kannada

Naanu thinnuththeeneನಾನು ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. thinnuththeeneತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆeat
