Kannada phrases: Ask Questions

Build question sentences. Here are 6 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “Who are you?” in Kannada

Niivu yaaruನೀವು ಯಾರು?

  1. Niivuನೀವುyou
  2. yaaruಯಾರು?who

How to say “What is this?” in Kannada

Idhu eenuಇದು ಏನು?

  1. Idhuಇದುthis
  2. eenuಏನು?what

How to say “Where are you?” in Kannada
