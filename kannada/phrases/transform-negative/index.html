Kannada phrases: Negative form practice

Build a positive sentence, then make it negative. Here are 4 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I eat” in Kannada

Naanu thinnuththeeneನಾನು ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. thinnuththeeneತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆeat

How to say “I do not eat” in Kannada

Naanu thinnuvudhillaನಾನು ತಿನ್ನುವುದಿಲ್ಲ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. thinnuvudhillaತಿನ್ನುವುದಿಲ್ಲdo not eat

How to say “I drink” in Kannada

  2. kudiyuththeeneಕುಡಿಯುತ್ತೇನೆdrink

How to say “I do not drink” in Kannada
