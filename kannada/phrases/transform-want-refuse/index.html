Kannada phrases: Want vs refuse

Build a request, then turn it into a polite refusal. Here are 4 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I want coffee” in Kannada

Nanage kaaphi beekuನನಗೆ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. kaaphiಕಾಫಿcoffee
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “I don't want coffee” in Kannada

Nanage kaaphi bedaನನಗೆ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇಡ

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. kaaphiಕಾಫಿcoffee
  3. bedaಬೇಡnot want

How to say “I want tea” in Kannada

Nanage chaa beekuನನಗೆ ಚಾ ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. chaaಚಾtea
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant
