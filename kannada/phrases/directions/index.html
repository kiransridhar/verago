Kannada phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around Karnataka. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “Where is the temple?” in Kannada

Gudi elli idheಗುಡಿ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ

  1. Gudiಗುಡಿtemple
  2. elliಎಲ್ಲಿwhere
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “Go straight” in Kannada

Neera hoogiನೇರ ಹೋಗಿ

  1. Neeraನೇರstraight
  2. hoogiಹೋಗಿgo

How to say “It is near here” in Kannada

Adhu illi haththira idheಅದು ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಹತ್ತಿರ ಇದೆ

  1. Adhuಅದುit
  2. illiಇಲ್ಲಿhere
  3. haththiraಹತ್ತಿರnear
  4. idheಇದೆis

How to say “It is far from here” in Kannada

Adhu illinda dhuura idheಅದು ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ ದೂರ ಇದೆ

  1. Adhuಅದುit
  2. illindaಇಲ್ಲಿಂದfrom here
  3. dhuuraದೂರfar
  4. idheಇದೆis

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Kannada

Aaspathre elli idheಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ?

  1. Aaspathreಆಸ್ಪತ್ರೆhospital
  2. elliಎಲ್ಲಿwhere
  3. idheಇದೆ?is

How to say “Turn right here” in Kannada

Illi balagade thirugiಇಲ್ಲಿ ಬಲಗಡೆ ತಿರುಗಿ

  1. Illiಇಲ್ಲಿhere
  2. balagadeಬಲಗಡೆto the right
  3. thirugiತಿರುಗಿturn
