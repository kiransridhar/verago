Kannada phrases: Weather & Small Talk

Chat about weather and the day. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Kannada

Ivaththu male baruththidheಇವತ್ತು ಮಳೆ ಬರುತ್ತಿದೆ

  1. Ivaththuಇವತ್ತುtoday
  2. maleಮಳೆrain
  3. baruththidheಬರುತ್ತಿದೆis coming

How to say “It is very hot” in Kannada

Thumbaa bisilu idheತುಂಬಾ ಬಿಸಿಲು ಇದೆ

  1. Thumbaaತುಂಬಾvery
  2. bisiluಬಿಸಿಲುsunshine
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “It is cold outside” in Kannada

Horage chali idheಹೊರಗೆ ಚಳಿ ಇದೆ

  1. Horageಹೊರಗೆoutside
  2. chaliಚಳಿcold
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “Tomorrow is a holiday” in Kannada

Naale rajaa idheನಾಳೆ ರಜಾ ಇದೆ

  1. Naaleನಾಳೆtomorrow
  2. rajaaರಜಾholiday
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “The sky is beautiful” in Kannada

Aakaasha sundharavaagidheಆಕಾಶ ಸುಂದರವಾಗಿದೆ

  1. Aakaashaಆಕಾಶsky
  2. sundharavaagidheಸುಂದರವಾಗಿದೆis beautiful

How to say “What a nice day!” in Kannada

Enthaha chendadha dhinaಎಂಥಹ ಚೆಂದದ ದಿನ

  1. Enthahaಎಂಥಹwhat a
  2. chendadhaಚೆಂದದlovely
  3. dhinaದಿನday

How to say “Take an umbrella” in Kannada

  1. Kodeಕೊಡೆumbrella
  2. thagolliತಗೊಳ್ಳಿtake
