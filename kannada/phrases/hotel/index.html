Kannada phrases: At the Hotel

Phrases for checking in and staying. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I need a room” in Kannada

Nanage ruum beekuನನಗೆ ರೂಮ್ ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. ruumರೂಮ್room
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “How much for one night?” in Kannada

Ondhu raathrige eshtuಒಂದು ರಾತ್ರಿಗೆ ಎಷ್ಟು?

  1. Ondhuಒಂದುone
  2. raathrigeರಾತ್ರಿಗೆfor night
  3. eshtuಎಷ್ಟು?how much

How to say “Where is the room?” in Kannada

Ruum elli idheರೂಮ್ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ

  1. Ruumರೂಮ್room
  2. elliಎಲ್ಲಿwhere
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “The key please” in Kannada

Dhayavittu kiili kodiದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಕೀಲಿ ಕೊಡಿ

  1. Dhayavittuದಯವಿಟ್ಟುplease
  2. kiiliಕೀಲಿkey
  3. kodiಕೊಡಿgive

How to say “AC is not working” in Kannada

Esi kelasa maaduththillaಎಸಿ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ

  1. EsiಎಸಿAC
  2. kelasaಕೆಲಸwork
  3. maaduththillaಮಾಡುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲis not doing

How to say “I want to check out” in Kannada

Nanage chek aut maadabeekuನನಗೆ ಚೆಕ್ ಔಟ್ ಮಾಡಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. chek autಚೆಕ್ ಔಟ್check out
  3. maadabeekuಮಾಡಬೇಕುmust do

How to say “Is breakfast free?” in Kannada

Upahaara uchitha idheyaaಉಪಹಾರ ಉಚಿತ ಇದೆಯಾ?

  1. Upahaaraಉಪಹಾರbreakfast
  2. uchithaಉಚಿತfree
  3. idheyaaಇದೆಯಾ?is it?
