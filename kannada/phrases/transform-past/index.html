Kannada phrases: Past tense practice

Build a present-tense sentence, then transform it to the past. Here are 4 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I eat” in Kannada

Naanu thinnuththeeneನಾನು ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. thinnuththeeneತಿನ್ನುತ್ತೇನೆeat

How to say “I ate” in Kannada

Naanu thindheನಾನು ತಿಂದೆ

  1. NaanuನಾನುI
  2. thindheತಿಂದೆate

How to say “I drink” in Kannada

  2. kudiyuththeeneಕುಡಿಯುತ್ತೇನೆdrink
