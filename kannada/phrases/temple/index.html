Kannada phrases: At the Temple

Sentences for visiting sacred places. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “This temple is beautiful” in Kannada

Ii gudi sundharavaagidheಈ ಗುಡಿ ಸುಂದರವಾಗಿದೆ

  1. Iiಈthis
  2. gudiಗುಡಿtemple
  3. sundharavaagidheಸುಂದರವಾಗಿದೆis beautiful

How to say “Where is the prayer hall?” in Kannada

Puujaa mantapa elli idheಪೂಜಾ ಮಂಟಪ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ?

  1. Puujaaಪೂಜಾprayer
  2. mantapaಮಂಟಪhall
  3. elliಎಲ್ಲಿwhere
  4. idheಇದೆ?is

How to say “I want flowers” in Kannada

Nanage huuvu beekuನನಗೆ ಹೂವು ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. huuvuಹೂವುflowers
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “Remove your shoes” in Kannada

Nimma chappali thegeyiriನಿಮ್ಮ ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ ತೆಗೆಯಿರಿ

  1. Nimmaನಿಮ್ಮyour
  2. chappaliಚಪ್ಪಲಿfootwear
  3. thegeyiriತೆಗೆಯಿರಿremove

How to say “What time is the pooja?” in Kannada

Puuje yaavaaga idheಪೂಜೆ ಯಾವಾಗ ಇದೆ

  1. Puujeಪೂಜೆpooja
  2. yaavaagaಯಾವಾಗwhen
  3. idheಇದೆis

How to say “Give me prasad” in Kannada

Dhayavittu prasaadha kodiದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಪ್ರಸಾದ ಕೊಡಿ

  1. Dhayavittuದಯವಿಟ್ಟುplease
  2. prasaadhaಪ್ರಸಾದprasad
  3. kodiಕೊಡಿgive

How to say “This is very old” in Kannada

Idhu thumbaa haleyadhuಇದು ತುಂಬಾ ಹಳೆಯದು

  1. Idhuಇದುthis
  2. thumbaaತುಂಬಾvery
  3. haleyadhuಹಳೆಯದುold one
