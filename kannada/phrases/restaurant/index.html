Kannada phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and drinks like a local. Here are 7 Kannada sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Kannada says it.

How to say “I want coffee” in Kannada

Nanage kaaphi beekuನನಗೆ ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. kaaphiಕಾಫಿcoffee
  3. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “Give me the menu” in Kannada

Dhayavittu menu kodiದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಮೆನು ಕೊಡಿ

  1. Dhayavittuದಯವಿಟ್ಟುplease
  2. menuಮೆನುmenu
  3. kodiಕೊಡಿgive

How to say “This food is good” in Kannada

Ii uuta chennaagidheಈ ಊಟ ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆ

  1. Iiಈthis
  2. uutaಊಟfood
  3. chennaagidheಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದೆis good

How to say “I want rice and yogurt” in Kannada

Nanage anna mosaru beekuನನಗೆ ಅನ್ನ ಮೊಸರು ಬೇಕು

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. annaಅನ್ನrice
  3. mosaruಮೊಸರುyogurt
  4. beekuಬೇಕುwant

How to say “How much is the bill?” in Kannada

Bil eshtu aayithuಬಿಲ್ ಎಷ್ಟು ಆಯಿತು?

  1. Bilಬಿಲ್bill
  2. eshtuಎಷ್ಟುhow much
  3. aayithuಆಯಿತು?became

How to say “I do not want spicy” in Kannada

Nanage khaara beedaನನಗೆ ಖಾರ ಬೇಡ

  1. Nanageನನಗೆto me
  2. khaaraಖಾರspicy
  3. beedaಬೇಡnot want

How to say “Please give me tea” in Kannada

Dhayavittu chaa kodiದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಚಾ ಕೊಡಿ

  1. Dhayavittuದಯವಿಟ್ಟುplease
  2. chaaಚಾtea
  3. kodiಕೊಡಿgive
