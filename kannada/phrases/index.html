Kannada phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

KannadaEnglish
🔁 Innondhu sala heeliಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಸಲ ಹೇಳಿ Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Nidhaanavaagi maathaadiನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಿ Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Kshamisi, keelisalillaಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, ಕೇಳಿಸಲಿಲ್ಲ Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Nanage artha aaguvudhillaನನಗೆ ಅರ್ಥ ಆಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Idhu yenu?ಇದು ಏನು? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🪔 Idhakke Kannada-dalli yenantaare?ಇದಕ್ಕೆ ಕನ್ನಡದಲ್ಲಿ ಏನಂತಾರೆ? What's this called in Kannada?Ask for the Kannada word when you only know the English.
📚 Naanu Kannada kaliyuththidhdheneನಾನು ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ I'm learning KannadaSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Nanage Kannada svalpa baruththeನನಗೆ ಕನ್ನಡ ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ಬರುತ್ತೆ I know a little KannadaHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Baredhu kodiಬರೆದು ಕೊಡಿ Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 English-nalli heeliಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಹೇಳಿ In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
