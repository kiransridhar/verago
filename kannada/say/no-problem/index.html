How to say “No problem” in Kannada

“No problem” in Kannada is Thondhare illa (ತೊಂದರೆ ಇಲ್ಲ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Thondhare illaತೊಂದರೆ ಇಲ್ಲ

It is one of the 15 words in Kannada polite phrases. The letters under the word are Kannada script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
