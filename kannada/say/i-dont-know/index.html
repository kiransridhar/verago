How to say “I don’t know” in Kannada

“I don’t know” in Kannada is Goththilla (ಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Goththillaಗೊತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ

Say it in 3 parts: Go · ththi · lla

It is one of the 15 words in Kannada everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Kannada script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
