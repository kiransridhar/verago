How to say “Hello” in Kannada

“Hello” in Kannada is Namaskaara (ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Namaskaaraನಮಸ್ಕಾರ

Say it in 4 parts: Na · ma · skaa · ra

It is one of the 15 words in Kannada greetings. The letters under the word are Kannada script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
