How to say “What’s new?” in Kannada

“What’s new?” in Kannada is Eenu visheesha (ಏನು ವಿಶೇಷ). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Eenu visheeshaಏನು ವಿಶೇಷ

It is one of the 15 words in Kannada family phrases. The letters under the word are Kannada script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
