Learn Kannada ಕನ್ನಡ

Kannada is the language of Karnataka in south India, spoken by more than 40 million people, from Bengaluru and Mysuru to families all over the world. It has its own beautiful rounded script and a literary history of over a thousand years.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Kannada words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Kannada script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
