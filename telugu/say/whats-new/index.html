How to say “What’s new?” in Telugu

“What’s new?” in Telugu is Emiti visheshaalu (ఏమిటి విశేషాలు). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Emiti visheshaaluఏమిటి విశేషాలు

It is one of the 15 words in Telugu family phrases. The letters under the word are Telugu script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
