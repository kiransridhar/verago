How to say “Come in” in Telugu

“Come in” in Telugu is Lopaliki randi (లోపలికి రండి). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Lopaliki randiలోపలికి రండి

It is one of the 15 words in Telugu polite phrases. The letters under the word are Telugu script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
