How to say “I don’t know” in Telugu

“I don’t know” in Telugu is Theliyadhu (తెలియదు). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Theliyadhuతెలియదు

Say it in 4 parts: The · li · ya · dhu

It is one of the 15 words in Telugu everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Telugu script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
