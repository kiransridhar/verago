How to say “Good night” in Telugu

“Good night” in Telugu is Shubha raathri (శుభ రాత్రి). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Shubha raathriశుభ రాత్రి

It is one of the 15 words in Telugu greetings. The letters under the word are Telugu script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
