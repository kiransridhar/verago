Telugu is the language of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, spoken by more than 80 million people, and one of the fastest-growing languages in American homes. It has its own rounded script, closely related to Kannada.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Telugu words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Telugu script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
