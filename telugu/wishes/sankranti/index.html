How to say “Happy Sankranti” in Telugu

Happy Sankranti in Telugu is Sankraanthi shubhaakaankshalu (సంక్రాంతి శుభాకాంక్షలు). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Sankranti is the biggest festival of the year for many Telugu families: three days in the middle of January called Bhogi, Sankranti and Kanuma. Doorsteps are decorated with muggulu (patterns drawn on the ground) and the sky fills with kites.

“Happy Sankranti”

Sankraanthi shubhaakaankshaluసంక్రాంతి శుభాకాంక్షలు

  1. Sankraanthiసంక్రాంతిSankranti
  2. shubhaakaankshaluశుభాకాంక్షలుgood wishes
