Telugu phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

TeluguEnglish
🔁 Inkosaari cheppandiఇంకోసారి చెప్పండి Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Nemmadhigaa maatlaadandiనెమ్మదిగా మాట్లాడండి Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Kshaminchandi, vinapadaledhuక్షమించండి, వినపడలేదు Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Naaku artham kaavatledhuనాకు అర్థం కావట్లేదు I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Idhi emiti?ఇది ఏమిటి? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🪔 Dheenini Telugu-lo emantaaru?దీనిని తెలుగులో ఏమంటారు? What's this called in Telugu?Ask for the Telugu word when you only know the English.
📚 Nenu Telugu nerchukuntunnaanuనేను తెలుగు నేర్చుకుంటున్నాను I'm learning TeluguSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Naaku Telugu koncham vachchuనాకు తెలుగు కొంచెం వచ్చు I know a little TeluguHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Raasi ivvandiరాసి ఇవ్వండి Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 English-lo cheppandiఇంగ్లీషులో చెప్పండి In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
