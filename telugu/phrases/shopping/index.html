Telugu phrases: Shopping & Travel

Sentences for real-world situations. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I want to go” in Telugu

Nenu vellaaliనేను వెళ్ళాలి

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. vellaaliవెళ్ళాలిmust go

How to say “Please give me water” in Telugu

Dhayachesi neellu ivvandiదయచేసి నీళ్ళు ఇవ్వండి

  1. Dhayachesiదయచేసిplease
  2. neelluనీళ్ళుwater
  3. ivvandiఇవ్వండిgive

How to say “How much is this?” in Telugu

Idhi entha avuthundhiఇది ఎంత అవుతుంది?

  1. Idhiఇదిthis
  2. enthaఎంతhow much
  3. avuthundhiఅవుతుంది?becomes

How to say “I will come tomorrow” in Telugu

Nenu repu vasthaanuనేను రేపు వస్తాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. repuరేపుtomorrow
  3. vasthaanuవస్తానుwill come

How to say “Turn left” in Telugu

Edamaku thiragandiఎడమకు తిరగండి

  1. Edamakuఎడమకుto the left
  2. thiragandiతిరగండిturn

How to say “Stop here” in Telugu

  1. Ikkadaఇక్కడhere
  2. aapandiఆపండిstop

How to say “I ate rice today” in Telugu

Nenu ivaala annam thinnaanuనేను ఇవాళ అన్నం తిన్నాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. ivaalaఇవాళtoday
  3. annamఅన్నంrice
  4. thinnaanuతిన్నానుate
