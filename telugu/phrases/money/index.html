Telugu phrases: Money & ATM

Handle cash, cards, and the ATM. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “Where is the ATM?” in Telugu

ATM ekkada undhiఏటీఎం ఎక్కడ ఉంది?

  1. ATMఏటీఎంATM
  2. ekkadaఎక్కడwhere
  3. undhiఉంది?is

How to say “I need cash” in Telugu

Naaku nagadhu kaavaaliనాకు నగదు కావాలి

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. nagadhuనగదుcash
  3. kaavaaliకావాలిwant

How to say “Do you take card?” in Telugu

Meeru kaard theesukuntaaraaమీరు కార్డ్ తీసుకుంటారా?

  1. Meeruమీరుyou
  2. kaardకార్డ్card
  3. theesukuntaaraaతీసుకుంటారా?take?

How to say “Cash only” in Telugu

Nagadhu maathrameనగదు మాత్రమే

  1. Nagadhuనగదుcash
  2. maathrameమాత్రమేonly

How to say “Give me change” in Telugu

Chillara ivvandiచిల్లర ఇవ్వండి

  1. Chillaraచిల్లరchange
  2. ivvandiఇవ్వండిgive

How to say “This is too expensive” in Telugu

Idhi chaalaa khareedhuఇది చాలా ఖరీదు

  1. Idhiఇదిthis
  2. chaalaaచాలాvery
  3. khareedhuఖరీదుexpensive

How to say “Can you reduce the price?” in Telugu

Dhara konchem thagginchandiధర కొంచెం తగ్గించండి

  1. Dharaధరprice
  2. konchemకొంచెంa little
  3. thagginchandiతగ్గించండిreduce
