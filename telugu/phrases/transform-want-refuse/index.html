Telugu phrases: Want vs refuse

Build a request, then turn it into a polite refusal. Here are 4 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I want coffee” in Telugu

Naaku kaaphee kaavaaliనాకు కాఫీ కావాలి

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. kaapheeకాఫీcoffee
  3. kaavaaliకావాలిwant

How to say “I don't want coffee” in Telugu

Naaku kaaphee vaddhuనాకు కాఫీ వద్దు

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. kaapheeకాఫీcoffee
  3. vaddhuవద్దుnot want

How to say “I want tea” in Telugu

Naaku tee kaavaaliనాకు టీ కావాలి

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. teeటీtea
  3. kaavaaliకావాలిwant
