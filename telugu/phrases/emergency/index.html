Telugu phrases: Emergencies & Help

Get help when things go wrong. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Telugu

Dhayachesi sahaayam cheyandiదయచేసి సహాయం చేయండి

  1. Dhayachesiదయచేసిplease
  2. sahaayamసహాయంhelp
  3. cheyandiచేయండిdo

How to say “Call the police” in Telugu

How to say “I am lost” in Telugu
