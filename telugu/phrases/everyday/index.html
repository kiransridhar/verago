Useful sentences for daily life. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I am hungry” in Telugu

Naaku aakali vesthondhiనాకు ఆకలి వేస్తోంది

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. aakaliఆకలిhunger
  3. vesthondhiవేస్తోందిis striking
