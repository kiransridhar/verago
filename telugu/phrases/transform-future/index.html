Telugu phrases: Future tense practice

Build a present-tense sentence, then make it future. Here are 4 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I am eating” in Telugu

Nenu thintunnaanuనేను తింటున్నాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. thintunnaanuతింటున్నానుam eating

How to say “I will eat” in Telugu

Nenu thintaanuనేను తింటాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. thintaanuతింటానుwill eat
