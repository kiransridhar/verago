Telugu phrases: Weather & Small Talk

Chat about weather and the day. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Telugu

Ivaala varsham paduthondhiఇవాళ వర్షం పడుతోంది

  1. Ivaalaఇవాళtoday
  2. varshamవర్షంrain
  3. paduthondhiపడుతోందిis falling

How to say “It is very hot” in Telugu

Chaalaa endagaa undhiచాలా ఎండగా ఉంది

  1. Chaalaaచాలాvery
  2. endagaaఎండగాsunny
  3. undhiఉందిis
