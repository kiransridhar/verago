Telugu phrases: Daily Routine

Walk through your day in Telugu. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I wake up at 6” in Telugu

Nenu aaru gantalaki lesthaanuనేను ఆరు గంటలకి లేస్తాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. aaruఆరుsix
  3. gantalakiగంటలకిat o'clock
  4. lesthaanuలేస్తానుwake up

How to say “I drink coffee in morning” in Telugu

Nenu poddhuna kaaphee thaaguthaanuనేను పొద్దున కాఫీ తాగుతాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. poddhunaపొద్దునin the morning
  3. kaapheeకాఫీcoffee
  4. thaaguthaanuతాగుతానుdrink
