Telugu phrases: Feelings & Emotions

Express how you feel. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I am happy” in Telugu

Nenu santhoshamgaa unnaanuనేను సంతోషంగా ఉన్నాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. santhoshamgaaసంతోషంగాhappily
  3. unnaanuఉన్నానుam
