Telugu phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and drinks like a local. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I want coffee” in Telugu

Naaku kaaphee kaavaaliనాకు కాఫీ కావాలి

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. kaapheeకాఫీcoffee
  3. kaavaaliకావాలిwant

How to say “Give me the menu” in Telugu

Dhayachesi menoo ivvandiదయచేసి మెనూ ఇవ్వండి

  1. Dhayachesiదయచేసిplease
  2. menooమెనూmenu
  3. ivvandiఇవ్వండిgive

How to say “This food is good” in Telugu

Ee bhojanam baagundhiఈ భోజనం బాగుంది

  1. Eeఈthis
  2. bhojanamభోజనంmeal
  3. baagundhiబాగుందిis good

How to say “I want rice and yogurt” in Telugu

  2. annamఅన్నంrice
  3. peruguపెరుగుyogurt
  4. kaavaaliకావాలిwant

How to say “How much is the bill?” in Telugu
