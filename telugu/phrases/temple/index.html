Telugu phrases: At the Temple

Sentences for visiting sacred places. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “This temple is beautiful” in Telugu

Ee gudi andhamgaa undhiఈ గుడి అందంగా ఉంది

  1. Eeఈthis
  2. gudiగుడిtemple
  3. andhamgaaఅందంగాbeautiful
  4. undhiఉందిis

How to say “Where is the prayer hall?” in Telugu

Poojaa mandapam ekkada undhiపూజా మండపం ఎక్కడ ఉంది?

  1. Poojaaపూజాprayer
  2. mandapamమండపంhall
  3. ekkadaఎక్కడwhere
  4. undhiఉంది?is

How to say “I want flowers” in Telugu

Naaku poolu kaavaaliనాకు పూలు కావాలి

  1. Naakuనాకుto me
  2. pooluపూలుflowers
  3. kaavaaliకావాలిwant

How to say “Remove your shoes” in Telugu

Mee cheppulu theeyandiమీ చెప్పులు తీయండి

  1. Meeమీyour
  2. cheppuluచెప్పులుfootwear
  3. theeyandiతీయండిremove

How to say “What time is the pooja?” in Telugu

  1. Poojaపూజpooja
  2. eppuduఎప్పుడుwhen
  3. undhiఉందిis

How to say “Give me prasad” in Telugu

Dhayachesi prasaadham ivvandiదయచేసి ప్రసాదం ఇవ్వండి

  1. Dhayachesiదయచేసిplease
  2. prasaadhamప్రసాదంprasad
  3. ivvandiఇవ్వండిgive

How to say “This is very old” in Telugu

Idhi chaalaa paathadhiఇది చాలా పాతది

  1. Idhiఇదిthis
  2. chaalaaచాలాvery
