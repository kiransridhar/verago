Telugu phrases: At Work & Office

Meetings, coworkers, and office life. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I am busy now” in Telugu

Nenu ippudu bijeegaa unnaanuనేను ఇప్పుడు బిజీగా ఉన్నాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. ippuduఇప్పుడుnow
  3. bijeegaaబిజీగాbusy
  4. unnaanuఉన్నానుam

How to say “Let's meet at 5” in Telugu

  1. Aidhuఐదుfive
  2. gantalakiగంటలకిat o'clock
  3. kaludhaamకలుదాంlet's meet
