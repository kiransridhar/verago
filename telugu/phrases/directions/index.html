Telugu phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around Telangana and Andhra. Here are 7 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “Where is the temple?” in Telugu

Gudi ekkada undhiగుడి ఎక్కడ ఉంది

  1. Gudiగుడిtemple
  2. ekkadaఎక్కడwhere
  3. undhiఉందిis

How to say “Go straight” in Telugu

Nerugaa vellandiనేరుగా వెళ్ళండి

  1. Nerugaaనేరుగాstraight
  2. vellandiవెళ్ళండిgo

How to say “It is near here” in Telugu

Adhi ikkadiki dhaggara undhiఅది ఇక్కడికి దగ్గర ఉంది

  1. Adhiఅదిit
  2. ikkadikiఇక్కడికిto here
  3. dhaggaraదగ్గరnear
  4. undhiఉందిis

How to say “It is far from here” in Telugu

How to say “Turn right here” in Telugu
