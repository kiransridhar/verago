Telugu phrases: Negative form practice

Build a positive sentence, then make it negative. Here are 4 Telugu sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Telugu says it.

How to say “I eat” in Telugu

Nenu thintaanuనేను తింటాను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. thintaanuతింటానుeat

How to say “I do not eat” in Telugu

Nenu thinanuనేను తినను

  1. NenuనేనుI
  2. thinanuతిననుdo not eat

How to say “I drink” in Telugu
