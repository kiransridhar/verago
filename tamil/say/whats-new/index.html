How to say “What’s new?” in Tamil

“What’s new?” in Tamil is Enna vishesham (என்ன விசேஷம்). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Enna visheshamஎன்ன விசேஷம்

It is one of the 15 words in Tamil family phrases. The letters under the word are Tamil script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
