How to say “Welcome” in Tamil

“Welcome” in Tamil is Varaverpu (வரவேற்பு). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Varaverpuவரவேற்பு

Say it in 4 parts: Va · ra · ve · rpu

It is one of the 15 words in Tamil polite phrases. The letters under the word are Tamil script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
