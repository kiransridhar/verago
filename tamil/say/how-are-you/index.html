How to say “How are you?” in Tamil

“How are you?” in Tamil is Eppadi irukkireergal (எப்படி இருக்கிறீர்கள்). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Eppadi irukkireergalஎப்படி இருக்கிறீர்கள்

In the Verago app this is taught as “How are you”.

It is one of the 15 words in Tamil greetings. The letters under the word are Tamil script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
