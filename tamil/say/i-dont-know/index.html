How to say “I don’t know” in Tamil

“I don’t know” in Tamil is Theriyaadhu (தெரியாது). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Theriyaadhuதெரியாது

Say it in 4 parts: The · ri · yaa · dhu

It is one of the 15 words in Tamil everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Tamil script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
