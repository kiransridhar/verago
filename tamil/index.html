Learn Tamil தமிழ்

Tamil is one of the world's oldest living languages, spoken by more than 70 million people in Tamil Nadu, Sri Lanka, Singapore, Malaysia and Tamil families everywhere. It has its own script and a literature reaching back over two thousand years.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Tamil words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Tamil script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
