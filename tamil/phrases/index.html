Tamil phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

TamilEnglish
🔁 Meendum sollungaமீண்டும் சொல்லுங்க Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Medhuvaa pesungaமெதுவா பேசுங்க Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Mannikkavum, enaku kekkalaமன்னிக்கவும், எனக்கு கேக்கல Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Enakku puriyalaஎனக்கு புரியல I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Idhu enna?இது என்ன? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🪔 Idhukku Tamizhla enna peyar?இதுக்கு தமிழ்ல என்ன பெயர்? What's this called in Tamil?Ask for the Tamil word when you only know the English.
📚 Naan Tamizh kathukkirenநான் தமிழ் கத்துக்கிறேன் I'm learning TamilSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Enakku konjam Tamizh theriyumஎனக்கு கொஞ்சம் தமிழ் தெரியும் I know a little TamilHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Adhai ezhudhi kudukareengalaa?அதை எழுதி குடுக்கறீங்களா? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Englishla sollureengala please?இங்கிலீஷ்ல சொல்லுறீங்களா? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
