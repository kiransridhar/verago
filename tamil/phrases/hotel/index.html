Tamil phrases: At the Hotel

Check in and ask for help. Here are 5 Tamil sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Tamil says it.

How to say “I want one room” in Tamil

Enakku oru arai venumஎனக்கு ஒரு அறை வேணும்

  1. Enakkuஎனக்குto me
  2. oruஒருone
  3. araiஅறைroom
  4. venumவேணும்want

How to say “How much per night?” in Tamil

Oru iravukku evvalavuஒரு இரவுக்கு எவ்வளவு

  1. Oruஒருone
  2. iravukkuஇரவுக்குfor night
  3. evvalavuஎவ்வளவுhow much

How to say “Where is my room?” in Tamil

Ennoda arai engaஎன்னோட அறை எங்கே

  1. Ennodaஎன்னோடmy
  2. araiஅறைroom
  3. engaஎங்கேwhere

How to say “Bring breakfast please” in Tamil

Dayavu seidhu kaalai unavu kondu vaangaதயவு செய்து காலை உணவு கொண்டு வாங்க

  1. Dayavuதயவுplease
  2. seidhuசெய்து(kindly)
  3. kaalaiகாலைmorning
  4. unavuஉணவுfood
  5. konduகொண்டுtaking
  6. vaangaவாங்கcome

How to say “Wi-fi is not working” in Tamil

Wi-Fi velai seiyalaவைஃபை வேலை செய்யல

  1. Wi-FiவைஃபைWi-Fi
  2. velaiவேலைwork
  3. seiyalaசெய்யலnot doing
