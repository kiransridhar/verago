Tamil phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Tamil sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Tamil says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Tamil

Moththam evvalavu aachuமொத்தம் எவ்வளவு ஆச்சு

  1. Moththamமொத்தம்total
  2. evvalavuஎவ்வளவுhow much
  3. aachuஆச்சுbecame

How to say “I will pay by card” in Tamil

Naan card-la pay panniduvenநான் கார்ட்ல பே பண்ணிடுவேன்

  1. Naanநான்I
  2. card-laகார்ட்லby card
  3. pay panniduvenபே பண்ணிடுவேன்will pay

How to say “Cash is fine” in Tamil

Cash okayகேஷ் சரி

  1. Cashகேஷ்cash
  2. okayசரிokay

How to say “Give me the change” in Tamil

Sillarai kudungaசில்லறை குடுங்க

  1. Sillaraiசில்லறைchange
  2. kudungaகுடுங்கgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Tamil

Idhu romba vilai uyarndhadhuஇது ரொம்ப விலையுயர்ந்தது

  1. Idhuஇதுthis
  2. rombaரொம்பvery
  3. vilai uyarndhadhuவிலையுயர்ந்ததுhigh-priced
