Tamil greetings and wishes

How to wish family and friends in Tamil, for a message, a phone call or a card. Each greeting has audio, the Tamil script, what every word means, and a card you can print.

  • 🌾Happy Pongal in Tamilபொங்கல் வாழ்த்துக்கள்
  • 🎆Happy New Year in Tamilபுத்தாண்டு வாழ்த்துக்கள்
  • 🪔Happy Diwali in Tamilதீபாவளி வாழ்த்துக்கள்
  • 🎂Happy Birthday in Tamilபிறந்தநாள் வாழ்த்துக்கள்

A note on accuracy: these Tamil greetings were written with AI help and have not yet been checked by a native speaker. Spot something that sounds off? Please tell us at veragoapp@gmail.com.
