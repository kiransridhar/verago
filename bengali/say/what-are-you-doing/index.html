How to say “What are you doing?” in Bengali

“What are you doing?” in Bengali is Ki korchho? (কী করছ?). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Ki korchho?কী করছ?

It is one of the 15 words in Bengali family phrases. The letters under the word are Bengali script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
