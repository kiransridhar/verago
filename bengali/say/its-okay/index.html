How to say “It’s okay” in Bengali

“It’s okay” in Bengali is Thik achhe (ঠিক আছে). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Thik achheঠিক আছে

It is one of the 15 words in Bengali polite phrases. The letters under the word are Bengali script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
