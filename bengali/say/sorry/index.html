How to say “Sorry” in Bengali

“Sorry” in Bengali is Dukkhito (দুঃখিত). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Dukkhitoদুঃখিত

Say it in 3 parts: Du · kkhi · to

It is one of the 15 words in Bengali greetings. The letters under the word are Bengali script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
