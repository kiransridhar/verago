How to say “I don’t know” in Bengali

“I don’t know” in Bengali is Jani na (জানি না). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Jani naজানি না

It is one of the 15 words in Bengali everyday essentials. The letters under the word are Bengali script; the line above it shows the same word in English letters, so you can say it without reading the script.
