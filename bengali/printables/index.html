Free printable Bengali worksheets

Worksheets for weekend language classes and for families learning Bengali at home. Pick a topic and print. There is nothing to sign up for, and you may print as many copies as you like for your class or family.

Each topic prints as three pages: a word list (picture, the word in English letters, the word in Bengali script, and its meaning), picture cards to cut out, and a matching quiz with the answers in small print. Every page has a square code that opens the same words with sound, so children can hear how each one is said.
