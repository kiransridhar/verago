Bengali phrases: Family Talk

Talk about family members. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “My mother is here” in Bengali

Amar ma ekhaneআমার মা এখানে

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. maমাmother
  3. ekhaneএখানেhere

How to say “My father has gone to work” in Bengali

Amar baba kaje gechhenআমার বাবা কাজে গেছেন

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. babaবাবাfather
  3. kajeকাজেto work
  4. gechhenগেছেনhas gone

How to say “I have one brother” in Bengali

Amar ekjon bhai achheআমার একজন ভাই আছে

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. ekjonএকজনone (person)
  3. bhaiভাইbrother
  4. achheআছেis

How to say “My sister is younger” in Bengali

Amar bon chhotoআমার বোন ছোট

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. bonবোনsister
  3. chhotoছোটsmall

How to say “Grandmother tells stories” in Bengali

Dida golpo shonanদিদা গল্প শোনান

  1. Didaদিদাgrandmother
  2. golpoগল্পstories
  3. shonanশোনানtells (polite)
