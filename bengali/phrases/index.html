Bengali phrases to keep a conversation going

The sentences a learner needs most: how to ask someone to repeat, slow down, or tell you the word for something.

BengaliEnglish
🔁 Abar bolunআবার বলুন Say it againWhen you missed what they said.
🐢 Aste aste bolunআস্তে আস্তে বলুন Speak slowly pleaseWhen the speaker is going too fast.
👂 Maf korben, shunte painiমাফ করবেন, শুনতে পাইনি Sorry, I didn't hear thatPolite recovery when something was inaudible.
🤔 Ami bujhte parchhi naআমি বুঝতে পারছি না I don't understandThe all-purpose "you lost me" phrase.
❓ Eta ki?এটা কী? What is this?Point at something you don't know the word for.
🧡 Eke Banglay ki bole?একে বাংলায় কী বলে? What's this called in Bengali?Ask for the Bengali word when you only know the English.
📚 Ami Bangla shikhchhiআমি বাংলা শিখছি I'm learning BengaliSets expectations — most native speakers will slow down and help.
🤏 Ami olpo Bangla janiআমি অল্প বাংলা জানি I know a little BengaliHonest disclaimer — invites patience.
✍️ Apni ki likhe dite paren?আপনি কি লিখে দিতে পারেন? Could you write it down?When the spoken form is hard to catch but you can read.
🆘 Ingrejite bolben?ইংরেজিতে বলবেন? In English please?The escape hatch when nothing else is working.
