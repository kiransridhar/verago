Check in and ask for help. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I want one room” in Bengali

Amar ekta ghor chaiআমার একটা ঘর চাই

  1. Amarআমারto me
  2. ektaএকটাone
  3. ghorঘরroom
  4. chaiচাইwant

How to say “How much per night?” in Bengali

Ek rater bhara koto?এক রাতের ভাড়া কত?

  1. Ekএকone
  2. raterরাতেরnight's
  3. bharaভাড়াrent
  4. koto?কত?how much

How to say “Where is my room?” in Bengali

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. ghorঘরroom

How to say “Bring breakfast please” in Bengali

Jolkhabarta niye ashunজলখাবারটা নিয়ে আসুন

  1. Jolkhabartaজলখাবারটাthe breakfast
  2. niyeনিয়েtaking
  3. ashunআসুনplease come

How to say “Wi-fi is not working” in Bengali

  1. Wifiওয়াইফাইwi-fi
  2. kajকাজwork
  3. korchheকরছেis doing
  4. naনাnot
