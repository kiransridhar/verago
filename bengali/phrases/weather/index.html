Bengali phrases: Weather Talk

Talk about the weather. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “It is raining today” in Bengali

Aj brishti hoccheআজ বৃষ্টি হচ্ছে

  1. Ajআজtoday
  2. brishtiবৃষ্টিrain
  3. hoccheহচ্ছেis happening

How to say “The sun is hot” in Bengali

Rod khub koraরোদ খুব কড়া

  1. Rodরোদsunshine
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. koraকড়াstrong

How to say “It is cold here” in Bengali

Ekhane thandaএখানে ঠান্ডা

  1. Ekhaneএখানেhere
  2. thandaঠান্ডাcold

How to say “There is a strong wind” in Bengali

Jore hawa diccheজোরে হাওয়া দিচ্ছে

  1. Joreজোরেstrongly
  2. hawaহাওয়াwind
  3. diccheদিচ্ছেis blowing

How to say “The sky is blue” in Bengali

Akash nilআকাশ নীল

  1. Akashআকাশsky
  2. nilনীলblue
