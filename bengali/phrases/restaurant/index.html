Bengali phrases: At the Restaurant

Order food and pay the bill. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I want one tea” in Bengali

Ek kap cha chaiএক কাপ চা চাই

  1. Ekএকone
  2. kapকাপcup
  3. chaচাtea
  4. chaiচাইwant

How to say “The food is delicious” in Bengali

Khabarta khub shushaduখাবারটা খুব সুস্বাদু

  1. Khabartaখাবারটাthe food
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. shushaduসুস্বাদুtasty

How to say “Bring some water please” in Bengali

Ektu jol anunএকটু জল আনুন

  1. Ektuএকটুa little
  2. jolজলwater
  3. anunআনুনplease bring

How to say “Bill please” in Bengali

Bilta dinবিলটা দিন

  1. Biltaবিলটাthe bill
  2. dinদিনplease give

How to say “No spicy food” in Bengali

Jhal khabar chai naঝাল খাবার চাই না

  1. Jhalঝালspicy
  2. khabarখাবারfood
  3. chaiচাইwant
  4. naনাnot
