Bengali phrases: At the Shop

Sentences for shopping. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “How much is this?” in Bengali

Etar dam koto?এটার দাম কত?

  1. Etarএটারof this
  2. damদামprice
  3. koto?কত?how much

How to say “Give me one mango” in Bengali

Amake ekta aam dinআমাকে একটা আম দিন

  1. Amakeআমাকেto me
  2. ektaএকটাone
  3. aamআমmango
  4. dinদিনgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Bengali

Eta khub damiএটা খুব দামি

  1. Etaএটাthis
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. damiদামিexpensive

How to say “Do you have rice?” in Bengali

Apnar kachhe chal achhe?আপনার কাছে চাল আছে?

  1. Apnarআপনারyour
  2. kachheকাছেnear
  3. chalচালrice (uncooked)
  4. achhe?আছে?is

How to say “I will take this” in Bengali

Ami eta neboআমি এটা নেব

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. etaএটাthis
  3. neboনেবwill take
