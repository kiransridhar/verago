Bengali phrases: Asking Directions

Find your way around. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “Where is the station?” in Bengali

Station kothay?স্টেশন কোথায়?

  1. Stationস্টেশনstation
  2. kothay?কোথায়?where

How to say “Go straight ahead” in Bengali

Shoja samne janসোজা সামনে যান

  1. Shojaসোজাstraight
  2. samneসামনেahead
  3. janযানgo

How to say “Turn left there” in Bengali

Okhane ba dike ghurunওখানে বাঁ দিকে ঘুরুন

  1. Okhaneওখানেthere
  2. baবাঁleft
  3. dikeদিকেtowards
  4. ghurunঘুরুনturn

How to say “It is far from here” in Bengali

Ota ekhan theke onek dureওটা এখান থেকে অনেক দূরে

  1. Otaওটাit
  2. ekhanএখানhere
  3. thekeথেকেfrom
  4. onekঅনেকvery
  5. dureদূরেfar

How to say “It is very close” in Bengali

Ota khub kachheওটা খুব কাছে

  1. Otaওটাit
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. kachheকাছেnear
