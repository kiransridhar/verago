Bengali phrases: Emergency!

Call for help when it counts. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “Help me please” in Bengali

Amake shahajjo korunআমাকে সাহায্য করুন

  1. Amakeআমাকেme
  2. shahajjoসাহায্যhelp
  3. korunকরুনplease do

How to say “Call the police” in Bengali

Pulishke phone korunপুলিশকে ফোন করুন

  1. Pulishkeপুলিশকেto the police
  2. phoneফোনphone
  3. korunকরুনplease do

How to say “Where is the hospital?” in Bengali

Hashpatal kothay?হাসপাতাল কোথায়?

  1. Hashpatalহাসপাতালhospital
  2. kothay?কোথায়?where

How to say “I am hurt” in Bengali

Ami chot peyechhiআমি চোট পেয়েছি

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. chotচোটinjury
  3. peyechhiপেয়েছিhave got

How to say “Please come quickly” in Bengali

Taratari ashunতাড়াতাড়ি আসুন

  1. Taratariতাড়াতাড়িquickly
  2. ashunআসুনplease come
