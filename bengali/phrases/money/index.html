Bengali phrases: Money Talk

Pay and count change. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “How much is the total?” in Bengali

Mot koto holo?মোট কত হল?

  1. Motমোটtotal
  2. kotoকতhow much
  3. holo?হল?became

How to say “I will pay by card” in Bengali

Ami carde deboআমি কার্ডে দেব

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. cardeকার্ডেby card
  3. deboদেবwill give

How to say “Cash is fine” in Bengali

Nogod takay cholbeনগদ টাকায় চলবে

  1. Nogodনগদcash
  2. takayটাকায়in money
  3. cholbeচলবেwill do

How to say “Give me the change” in Bengali

Khuchro taka dinখুচরো টাকা দিন

  1. Khuchroখুচরোloose
  2. takaটাকাmoney
  3. dinদিনgive

How to say “It is too expensive” in Bengali

Eta khub damiএটা খুব দামি

  1. Etaএটাthis
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. damiদামিexpensive
