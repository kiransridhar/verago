Bengali phrases: At the Office

Meetings and tasks at work. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I am busy now” in Bengali

Ami ekhon byastoআমি এখন ব্যস্ত

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. ekhonএখনnow
  3. byastoব্যস্তbusy

How to say “Please send the email” in Bengali

Emailta pathiye dinইমেলটা পাঠিয়ে দিন

  1. Emailtaইমেলটাthe email
  2. pathiyeপাঠিয়েsending
  3. dinদিনplease give

How to say “The meeting is at 3” in Bengali

Meetingta tinteyমিটিংটা তিনটেয়

  1. Meetingtaমিটিংটাthe meeting
  2. tinteyতিনটেয়at three o'clock

How to say “I have a lot of work” in Bengali

Amar onek kaj achheআমার অনেক কাজ আছে

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. onekঅনেকa lot
  3. kajকাজwork
  4. achheআছেis

How to say “See you tomorrow” in Bengali

Kal dekha hobeকাল দেখা হবে

  1. Kalকালtomorrow
  2. dekhaদেখাmeeting
  3. hobeহবেwill be
