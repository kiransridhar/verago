Bengali phrases: How I Feel

Talk about emotions. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I am happy today” in Bengali

Aj ami khushiআজ আমি খুশি

  1. Ajআজtoday
  2. amiআমিI
  3. khushiখুশিhappy

How to say “I am very tired” in Bengali

Ami khub klantoআমি খুব ক্লান্ত

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. khubখুবvery
  3. klantoক্লান্তtired

How to say “I am hungry” in Bengali

Amar khide peyechheআমার খিদে পেয়েছে

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. khideখিদেhunger
  3. peyechheপেয়েছেhas come

How to say “I am scared” in Bengali

Amar bhoy korchheআমার ভয় করছে

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. bhoyভয়fear
  3. korchheকরছেis doing

How to say “I love this” in Bengali

Amar eta khub bhalo lageআমার এটা খুব ভালো লাগে

  1. Amarআমারto me
  2. etaএটাthis
  3. khubখুবvery
  4. bhaloভালোgood
  5. lageলাগেfeels
