Bengali phrases: At the Temple

Visit a temple respectfully. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I am going to the temple” in Bengali

Ami mondire jacchiআমি মন্দিরে যাচ্ছি

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. mondireমন্দিরেto the temple
  3. jacchiযাচ্ছিam going

How to say “God bless you” in Bengali

Bhogoban tomar mongol korunভগবান তোমার মঙ্গল করুন

  1. BhogobanভগবানGod
  2. tomarতোমারyour
  3. mongolমঙ্গলwelfare
  4. korunকরুনmay do

How to say “Take off your shoes” in Bengali

Juto khulunজুতো খুলুন

  1. Jutoজুতোshoes
  2. khulunখুলুনtake off

How to say “It is a holy place” in Bengali

Eta ekta pobitro jaygaএটা একটা পবিত্র জায়গা

  1. Etaএটাthis
  2. ektaএকটাa
  3. pobitroপবিত্রholy
  4. jaygaজায়গাplace

How to say “I am praying” in Bengali

Ami prarthona korchhiআমি প্রার্থনা করছি

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. prarthonaপ্রার্থনাprayer
  3. korchhiকরছিam doing
