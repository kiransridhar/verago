Bengali phrases: Build Sentences

Tap words to form everyday sentences. Here are 5 Bengali sentences with audio. Under each one, every word is shown with its literal meaning, in the order Bengali says it.

How to say “I eat rice” in Bengali

Ami bhaat khaiআমি ভাত খাই

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. bhaatভাতrice
  3. khaiখাইeat

How to say “I drink water” in Bengali

Ami jol khaiআমি জল খাই

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. jolজলwater
  3. khaiখাইdrink (lit. eat)

How to say “I go to school” in Bengali

Ami schoole jaiআমি স্কুলে যাই

  1. AmiআমিI
  2. schooleস্কুলেto school
  3. jaiযাইgo

How to say “My name is Robi” in Bengali

Amar nam Robiআমার নাম রবি

  1. Amarআমারmy
  2. namনামname
  3. RobiরবিRobi

How to say “How are you” in Bengali

Apni kemon achhenআপনি কেমন আছেন

  1. Apniআপনিyou
  2. kemonকেমনhow
  3. achhenআছেনare
