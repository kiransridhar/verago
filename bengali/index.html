Bengali (Bangla) is spoken by more than 230 million people in West Bengal and Bangladesh, the language of Tagore and Nazrul. It has its own script, related to Devanagari.

Below are 75 everyday topics with 1125 Bengali words. Every word has its romanization (how to say it in English letters), the Bengali script, its meaning, and a 🔊 button to hear it.
