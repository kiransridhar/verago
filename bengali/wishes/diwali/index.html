How to say “Happy Diwali” in Bengali

Happy Diwali in Bengali is Shubho Dipaboli (শুভ দীপাবলি). Tap 🔊 to hear it.

Diwali, called Dipaboli in Bengali, is the festival of lights, in October or November. In Bengal the same night is Kali Puja, and homes are lit with lamps and candles.

“Happy Diwali”

Shubho Dipaboliশুভ দীপাবলি

  1. Shubhoশুভhappy
  2. DipaboliদীপাবলিDiwali

“Diwali wishes”

Dipabolir shubhechchhaদীপাবলির শুভেচ্ছা

  1. Dipabolirদীপাবলিরof Diwali
  2. shubhechchhaশুভেচ্ছাgood wishes
